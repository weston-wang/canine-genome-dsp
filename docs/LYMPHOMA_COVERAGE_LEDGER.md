# Lymphoma: did we cover every mechanism and escape, with potency and toxicity considered?

Technical record for the rebuild requested on 2026-09-30. Plain-language summary first; the evidence
tables follow. Every number in the tables is regenerated by `tests/test_lymphoma_ledger.py` and
`tests/test_lymphoma_grounded.py` from `src/canine_dsp/core/lymphoma_grounded.py`,
`lymphoma_coverage_ledger.py` and `lymphoma_grounded_inputs.py`. Not veterinary advice.

## The bar, quoted (CLAUDE.md rule 2)

- "make sure every mechanism and every escape is closed by either real data or rigorous model, potency,
  toxicity etc all need to be considered"
- "looking for 10+ years of durability"; "assuming early detection"
- "I'm not asking if it's been demonstrated, I know it's not." So **"not demonstrated in dogs" is not
  reported anywhere below as a finding.** Where dog data exist they are used as inputs; where they do
  not, the entry says what is missing to make the input a measurement.

Grading is at four strengths and they are kept apart (rule 5):

| grade | meaning |
|---|---|
| **derived coverage** | the mechanism is in the model and an agent covers it by pathway position, display or efflux. A rigorous model of *who can reach what*, silent on *how hard*. |
| **STRICT** | the agent's kill rate is derived from a measured potency and a measured exposure, or measured in vivo. |
| **OUTCOME** | the benefit is a measured clinical outcome (or the regimen sum is calibrated to one), but this agent's own kill rate is not measured. |
| **ASSUMED** | the closure holds only if an unmeasured potency holds. |

## In plain words

**1. Mechanisms.** The list of ways the cancer can escape is more complete than it was, and it is still a
list. The earlier work had 11. Checking the canine literature added **two that were missing**: the tumour
hiding from the immune system by losing the molecules that present its antigens (low MHC-II predicts
relapse in 160 dogs), and DNA repair undoing lomustine. It also turned up mechanisms that are *not*
separate escapes but change the answer: the dog's own immune system rejecting a mouse-derived CAR-T
(measured, and it caps how long that treatment lasts), and the fact that some escape lesions are present
from the very start in a large share of dogs (TP53 in about 26% of B-cell cases, TRAF3 in about 58%), so
finding the cancer earlier cannot remove them. All 13 lineages have at least one agent in the model that
reaches them.

**2. Strength of the closure.** Being covered is not being killed. Only **three** agents have a kill rate
that is measured or derived from measurements: verdinexor, the dog anti-CD20 antibody, and (T-cell only,
partly) venetoclax. **No combination of only those closes every escape.** The smallest combination that
does, using only agents whose potency is derived, measured or outcome-calibrated, is:

- **B-cell, needs a trial-stage antibody:** doxorubicin + canine anti-CD20 antibody + verdinexor. In the
  model it clears every lineage by about day 51 with no organ axis over budget (tightest axis has 25%
  room). It has no single point of failure. **One escape (TP53) closes on measured potency by only
  +0.009/day, which is inside the noise**, and verdinexor's derived kill (0.27/day) is not matched by its
  clinical result (37% response, median 18 days), so this is a model result, not a demonstration.
- **B-cell with only licensed or off-label agents:** nothing whose potency is measured, derived or
  outcome-calibrated clears everything. What does clear it (for example doxorubicin + prednisolone +
  hydroxychloroquine, +0.108) closes the drug-pump escapes **only on assumed potency**.
- **T-cell:** doxorubicin + venetoclax (+0.008, thin) or doxorubicin + vincristine + venetoclax (+0.060).
  Venetoclax is the single point of failure for the pump escapes; its exposure in dogs is second-hand.
- **The brain and spinal cord (CNS) sanctuary is not closed at any evidence grade.** With only licensed or
  off-label agents nothing clears it. For B-cell the model clears it only with the trial-stage CD20 CAR-T
  plus two radiation courses, on four assumed potencies. T-cell needs an agent that does not exist.

**3. Toxicity changed the answer in specific places.** Organ budgets and how long each drug can be given
were added. Doxorubicin is capped near 6 doses by heart toxicity, lomustine near 4 by liver, prednisolone
at full dose near 150 days, and CAR-T lasts about 50 days in dogs because they make antibodies against
the mouse-derived part. As a result: every earlier "minimal" two-drug closing regimen **relapses when a
drug has to stop**; the earlier CNS B-cell closure relapses when the CAR-T ends; and the earlier
T-cell "most robust" regimen is over budget on bone marrow and has to be cut.

**4. So, to the question:** *every mechanism the literature raised is in the model and reached by an
agent; not every escape is closed by real data or a rigorous model at the potency level.* The pump and
apoptosis escapes in the off-label B-cell regimen, and the whole CNS sanctuary, close only if potencies
that have never been measured hold.

**A discrepancy found in the HS dormancy module (not changed on the HS branch).** Its margin is
`sum(kill_from(a)) - growth * cycling_fraction` with `kill_from = effective_kill * duty` for a
division-gated agent. `effective_kill` already contains duty, so duty is counted twice, and a
division-gated agent is credited at full strength against a pool of which only the awake fraction f can be
killed. Solving the two-state pool exactly (`tests/test_lymphoma_grounded.py` checks against an
independent eigenvalue) gives division-gated kill weighted by f. At f = 0.2 and a division-gated kill
of 0.05/day the HS formula says +0.032 (closes) while the pool still grows at +0.008/day. A second
finding while testing: with slow switching and no non-division-gated agent the dormant reservoir drains
only as fast as cells wake, so a division-gated-only regimen can "close" so slowly that the clock rules
it out. The decision therefore uses the exact rate, worst-cased over a switching-rate sweep. **The old
lymphoma persister "wall" is the corner f = 1, r = 1 of the new model**, and the sweep shows it is the only
corner where the best anchored regimen relapses (§6). f and r are unmeasured in canine lymphoma, so
they are swept, not assumed.

The plasma-concentration-versus-in-vitro-IC50 method is a **lower bound** for tissue-accumulating
cytotoxics: for doxorubicin and vincristine it gives about 0.24 and 0.37 e-folds of kill per dose against
about 1.9 e-folds of regrowth per 21-day cycle, which cannot reproduce CHOP's known activity (98%
response, PMID 26279153). So the CHOP potencies stay calibrated to the regimen outcome and are graded
REGIMEN-CALIBRATED, never STRICT. Cyclophosphamide's active metabolite differs 87-fold between two
laboratories (0.14 vs 12.19 uM, CLBL-1), so no derivation is defensible.

Hinges (an unmeasured potency turned into a testable threshold):

- **Hydroxychloroquine** closes only if its canine-lymphoma IC50 is at or below about **100 uM** (tumour
  concentration about 31 uM, 100 times plasma, PMID 24991836). No canine-lymphoma IC50 was found.
- **Venetoclax on T-cell** closes at 1.2 mg/kg/day if 1% of the drug is free, or 12.3 mg/kg/day if 0.1%
  is free, both inside the 2 to 20 mg/kg range beagles tolerated for up to 9 months. The exposure
  (AUC 73.6 ug.h/mL at 20 mg/kg) is quoted in PMID 36433867 without a primary citation and the free
  fraction was not found. On B-cell it does not close at any dose (EC50 mean 288 uM).
- **Prednisolone** is a bracket, 0.0098 to 0.144/day, from two canine measurements that disagree about
  15-fold. The catalogue's 0.10 is neither derived nor contradicted.

## 7. Checks against reality and against v1

- **Regression:** for the regimens v1 recorded, the grounded model reproduces v1's worst margins exactly
  (+0.2297 systemic B, +0.027 CNS B), so the port is consistent.
- **Calibration:** CHOP alone at a clinically obvious burden (1e11 cells) is predicted to give response
  then relapse (bulk regrows after day 126, pump lineages after day 150) against a real median PFS of
  176 days (PMID 26279153) and about 10% alive at 2 years (PMID 21320018). It is close to the edge: with
  prednisolone at the top of its measured bracket (0.144) the model would predict a cure, which real dogs
  contradict.
- **Consolidation:** adding low-dose-rate half-body irradiation to CHOP at the clinical burden removes the
  pump-lineage relapses. Real data: with L-CHOP, first-remission 39% vs 8.7% at 2 years and 18% vs 4.4% at
  5 years (n = 75 vs 115, PMID 42525883). The old model said a one-time consolidation adds nothing; the
  cause was averaging a 28-day course over a year, which the clock no longer does. The deterministic
  model gives cure or relapse, not fractions, so the size of the real effect is not reproduced.
- **What v1 got that changes:** every v1 *minimal* closing regimen (two to four agents at +0.0097)
  relapses under the clock; v1's CNS B-cell closure relapses when the CAR-T ends at day 50; v1's T-cell
  "most robust" regimen is over budget on bone marrow (headroom -0.10) and must be cut, lowering its
  margin from +0.200 to +0.177.

## 8. Limits of this rebuild

- **Loads are summed as if every agent were given at once.** Real practice sequences induction and
  consolidation. Adding TBI + transplant to full CHOP puts bone marrow at 1.5, forces a cut on everything
  and turns an early-burden cure into a relapse; a scheduling layer would not stack them. Not built.
- **Mean-field clock.** It does not represent stochastic extinction of a few cells, and it clears only
  lineages present at diagnosis (seeding rates are assumed, only the existence of each lesion is
  measured). New escapes arising during therapy are not in the clock; the Monte Carlo engine has them.
- **Toxicity budgets are ordinal judgements** and give headroom, not prediction. Several are transfers
  (marked "NO" under measured in dogs).
- **Verdinexor:** derived kill 0.27/day (B) against a clinical median response duration of 18 days. The
  in-vitro IC50 for CLBL-1 differs 13-fold between laboratories (8.5 vs 108.6 nM).
- **The antibody's own contribution is not isolated** from the chemotherapy given with it; its potency is
  the depletion of normal B cells in blood, not of nodal tumour.
- **CD20 CAR-T potency in vivo is unmeasured**; the only in-vivo canine data show no objective response at
  the persistence achieved. The persistence-engineered CAR-T is a specification.
- Persister awake fraction f, retained tolerance r and switching rate are unmeasured in canine lymphoma.
- Genotype-tiered choice of agents (HS `genotype_tiered_durability.py`), delivery routes such as focused
  ultrasound, human-vs-dog target conservation, and per-combination prior art (HS `PRIOR_ART_COMBINATIONS.md`)
  were not built; see the inventory in `LYMPHOMA_STATUS.md`.
