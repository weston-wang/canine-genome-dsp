## 1. L-ASPARAGINASE / PEG-ASPARAGINASE  (record: EVALUATED, EXCLUDED; s.M of SWEEP_inhibitors_partial gives only no-benefit-as-CHOP-add-on, ADA, human CSF 24702187)
(a) potency. In vivo single agent, dogs: PEG-asp 10-30 IU/kg IP weekly alone, n=20 (8 chemo-resistant): CR 7, PR 7, NR 5; response duration 14-102 d, median 48 d (PMID 3567863, Cancer 1987) [NEW]. PEG-asp 10 IU/kg IM vs native 400 IU/kg IP, n=42 randomised: no difference in response/remission, no side effects with PEG vs 48% (anaphylaxis 9, vomiting 4, oedema 3, seizure 1, pancreatitis 1) with native (PMID 2145933) [NEW]. Compounded L-asp 10,000 IU/m2 SC + steroid n=73: ORR 80% (CR 24%) (PMID 27086733) [NEW]. L-asp+CCNU+pred relapsed: median TTP 63 d (PMID 17338160) [REC growth_bar]; continuous L-asp+CCNU+pred PMID 19678892 [NEW pmid, same protocol family].
   In vitro: canine lymphoid lines L-asp sensitivity tracks ASNS promoter methylation (PMID 24033105; IC50 values not in abstract, full text not open); 17-71 and OSW canine lines at 10 IU/mL x24 h lose proliferation and Asn is depleted (PMID 23738234; single dose, no IC50). Dose-response numbers: NOT OBTAINED.
(b) exposure. Beagle: PEG-asp 10 IU/kg -> plasma Asn depleted 9 d (all 7 dogs), 15 d (5), 21 d (3), 29 d (1); suppression at PEG-asp activity < 25 IU/L [sic, abstract wording] (PMID 35781075). Repeat doses (20, 40 IU/kg) shortened suppression to <9 d in 5/7 with anti-PEG/anti-asp antibodies. Dog PEG-asp PK scaled by BSA matches paediatric ALL (PMID 24934864). Human: serum activity >=0.1 IU/mL depletes CSF Asn (rhesus + 7 pts, PMID 6895481); to hold CSF Asn <1 uM median 0.44 IU/mL (PMID 34170389).
(c) kill k. The k = ln(1+C/IC50)/days formula does NOT apply (units IU/mL; kill is cytostatic-then-apoptotic, needs days of Asn <1 uM). Only an OUTCOME k: single agent median remission 48 d / ORR 70% (3567863) -> same band as other rescue lines (growth_bar floors 0.015-0.07). No sound exposure-derived k.
(d) division. Cytostatic first: protein-synthesis arrest then apoptosis (8485807 [REC]); TEL/AML1 leukaemic cells arrest in cell cycle (14724653). Dormant-cell evidence is NEGATIVE: L-asp alone inhibited glutamine metabolism in CML stem-like/dormant cells "without inducing predominant CML cell death"; death only after TKI (PMID 34863941) [NEW]; canine lines: amino-acid (arginine) deprivation sends checkpoint-competent cells to quiescence with full recovery, killing only checkpoint-defective lines (PMID 23365669) [NEW]; marrow MSC secrete asparagine and protect ALL (PMID 17380207, 34614505) though 22961784 found no increase in marrow Asn in patients. => no support for killing a dormant cell.
(e) pump. Enzyme (~140 kDa tetramer; PEG form larger): not a P-gp/BCRP/MRP substrate by class (no citation needed; not a membrane permeant). Pump-armour irrelevant.
(f) CSF/brain. Rhesus: PEG-asp 2500 IU/m2 IM: plasma Asn 14-47 uM -> <2 uM by 24 h; CSF Asn 4.7-13.6 -> <0.25 uM by 48 h, <0.25 uM for 10-14 d in 4/6 (PMID 8324873). Children: E. coli asp 10,000 IU/m2 IM x6-9: CSF Asn median 4.42 -> <0.04 uM, undetectable in 73.9% during therapy, rebounds after (PMID 10334545); PEG-asp CSF Asn <1 uM 15.3 d (1 dose) / 30.7 d (2 doses) (PMID 34170389); CNS relapse higher when CSF depletion shorter (p=0.049). Enzyme itself does NOT enter CSF (IT-injected asp cleared at bulk-flow rate, 6895481); effect is via systemic Asn depletion. Dog CSF Asn: NOT FOUND. => CSF access = DERIVED/TRANSFER (rhesus+human), real.
(g) dCK: independent (not a nucleoside).
(h) escapes: ASNS promoter demethylation/expression (24033105, canine lines), anti-drug antibodies (35781075; 23253146 REC), marrow-niche Asn, SLC1A3 (31523835, solid tumours).
(i) toxicity axis: hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis (native), pancreatitis (2145933; lipase 19436578), coagulopathy not seen in 62 dogs (2145933), hepatic. No marrow toxicity => cheap in the budget. Not cumulative but antibody-limited: repeat dosing shortens depletion.
GRADE: E5-brain NOT closable (division/proliferation-dependent kill, dormant-cell evidence negative). Body/brain bulk and E-escape coverage: OUTCOME (PEG-asp alone, n=20 dogs). Asn-depletion in CSF: TRANSFER. Kill rate: ASSUMED -> OUTCOME ~0.03-0.05/d at best.

## 2. RABACFOSADINE (Tanovea; record: IN MODEL, ASSUMED 0.200/d; s.M gives ADAL pathway, resting-cells-spared, RCT 34952995, "kinases NOT RETRIEVED", P-gp "NOT FOUND")
(a) potency. In vitro (Reiser 2008, PMID 18451250; numbers via search snippet of the paper, full text not accessible -> VERIFY): GS-9219 EC50 27-1,043 nM in activated lymphocytes/haematopoietic tumour lines; mitogen-stimulated B 42 nM, T 135 nM; QUIESCENT lymphocytes EC50 17.2 uM (127-fold less sensitive than proliferating, T). CEM: PMEG inhibits DNA synthesis 50% at 1 uM, 24 h (PMID 10570054). No canine-lymphoma-line IC50 retrieved (canine GS-343074/-424044 analogue paper 23672437 is a different compound). In vivo: RCT n=158: BORR 73.2% (CR 50.9%), PFS 82 vs 21 d (PMID 34952995) [REC]; naive trials PMID 32346934; 0.82 vs 1.0 mg/kg equal, ORR 74%, median PFI 108 d, CR 203 d (PMID 28891260) [NEW pmid].
(b) exposure (FDA FOI Summary NADA 141-545, 2021, read in full, [NEW numbers]): 1 mg/kg IV 30 min q21d x5. Parent t1/2 <0.5 h; cPrPMEDAP plasma t1/2 6 h; PMEG NOT detected in plasma; PBMC (0.82 mg/kg q2-3wk, median 5 dogs) cPrPMEDAP 131 nM, PMEG(pp) 1,420 nM; PBMC t1/2 cPrPMEDAP 25 h, PMEGpp 68.7 h (the last two from search summary of the FOI/label; FOI text I read gives 131 and 1,420 only). Protein binding: NOT FOUND.
(c) kill k. Prodrug: extracellular C_free/IC50 formula not usable (plasma parent gone in <2 h; active species is intracellular PMEGpp, t1/2 69 h). Derivable ONLY as a ratio: dormant/proliferating EC50 = 127x. If the clinically effective exposure sits at the proliferating-lymphoma EC50 band (27-1,043 nM, geometric mid ~170 nM), the same exposure is C/EC50_quiescent = 0.17/17.2 = 0.01 -> k_dormant = ln(1.01)/3 d ~ 0.003/d (range 0.0003-0.02/d for 1,043..27 nM). Versus proliferating k = OUTCOME. => DERIVED k_dormant ~0.003-0.02/d, far below the 0.06 brain bar.
(d) division. YES, preferential (PMEGpp inhibits pol alpha/delta; replicative; reduced G1 fraction, PMIDs 9224813, 10570054, 10087324; "preferential cytotoxicity in cells with high proliferation index", 19417014; resting lymphocytes 127x less sensitive, 18451250). EXCEPTION [NEW, mechanism-level]: in non-cycling CLL cells GS-9219 kills when DNA repair resynthesis is triggered (UV): PMEGpp incorporated during nucleotide-excision repair, repair inhibited, p53/Puma/Bax, and GS-9219 + DNA-damaging agents killed more than sum (PMID 19435836). So a DNA-damage partner (lomustine or thiotepa) could turn the quiescent-cell kill on; effect size in lymphoma NOT measured => ASSUMED.
(e) pumps. No direct study of PMEG/GS-9219 efflux found. [NEW] 23856525 (Toxicology 2013): PMEG double prodrug GS-9191 (close analogue of GS-9219) is a strong INHIBITOR of MDR1 and BCRP; ANPs enter via hOAT1; no efflux-substrate finding. Active species PMEGpp is a charged diphosphate (not a pump substrate by chemistry). Grade: not a substrate = TRANSFER from analogue (weak).
(f) CSF/brain: NOT FOUND (PubMed 'rabacfosadine/PMEG + CNS/brain/CSF' = 0 relevant hits; label/FOI silent). No canine CNS lymphoma response data. => ASSUMED access.
(g) dCK: INDEPENDENT [NEW, closes record's 'kinases NOT RETRIEVED']: PMEG (phosphonate, already carries the first phosphate-mimic) is phosphorylated by GMP kinase isoenzymes (efficiency 1.1% of GMP) (PMID 11108807); deamination by ADAL (23645737); no nucleoside kinase step. So defeating E2 (dCK loss) is by pathway: TRANSFER (human enzymology). Escape: ADAL loss (23645737), not dCK.
(h) named escapes: ADAL mutation (H286R, S180N); high-proliferation dependence; quiescence.
(i) toxicity axis. FOI: 120 dogs treated: SAEs 20% (24/120); pulmonary fibrosis 5/120 (4%; all fatal or euthanised; onset median day 87 after randomisation, i.e. after ~4 doses, at CR); dermatopathy 67/120 (56%), 6 SAE; GI/neutropenia mild; contraindicated Westies. Pulmonary fibrosis "idiosyncratic, unknown mechanism". RCT: PMID 34952995. Budget: pulmonary (irreversible, fatal) is the cap, ~4 doses.
GRADE: potency OUTCOME (RCT) for proliferating cells; dormant-cell kill DERIVED-low (0.003-0.02/d); CSF access ASSUMED; dCK-independence TRANSFER; division-gated stays TRUE. => does NOT close E5 alone. It does close T-brain E2 for bulk (not E5) only if brain access is shown (not).

## 3. LOMUSTINE / CCNU (record: IN MODEL ASSUMED 0.120/d; one canine line IC50 27 uM; NCI-60 7.9-18 uM + no P-gp cross-res 1.1x REC SWEEP_nongated B; outcome 38890811, 41914636 REC; MGMT 26130852 REC in escapes; CSF "NOT FOUND" for dog)
(a) potency. Human lymphoid NCI-60 48 h GI50 7.9/13/18 uM (MOLT-4/CCRF-CEM/SR) [REC]; canine line 27 uM [REC, ledger]; 2/5 canine lymphoma lines more resistant, MGMT-inhibitor-sensitised (PMID 26130852) [REC]. Canine 17-71 low MGMT activity (~90 fmol/mg) and GL-1 none, hepatocytes 250-350 fmol/mg (PMID 29061931) [NEW]; DNA adducts only detectable at >=10 ug/mL (~42 uM) after 4 h; O6-hydroxyethyl adduct only at 50 ug/mL, 2-3x lower in hepatocytes than lymphoma lines (29061931) [NEW]. In vivo: first-line n=30 RR 87% (CR 50%), TTP 42 d, MST 90 d (PMID 38890811) [REC]; mediastinal T n=70 PFS 132 d (41914636) [REC]; rescue with L-asp+pred TTP 63 d (17338160) [REC].
(b) exposure. Dog: parent undetectable in plasma; trans-4-OH-CCNU Cmax ~325 ng/mL (1.3 uM, MW 250.7) at 1 h (web snippet of Vet Sci 2014;1:159, Dirikolu; MDPI blocked -> UNVERIFIED); metabolite t1/2 16 h-2 d (label, human); LC method PMID 20040134. Human 130 mg/m2: parent not detectable; trans+cis-4-OH total peak 0.8-0.9 ug/mL (3.4 uM) at 2-4 h, t1/2 1.3-2.9 h, "at the lower limit of exposures required for significant antineoplastic activity" (PMID 3971475) [NEW]; high-dose 15 mg/kg: peak 1.56 (trans) + 1.10 (cis) mg/L, t1/2 3.1-3.5 h (PMID 8765435) [NEW]. Protein binding: not in label.
(c) kill k (my arithmetic). Human-dose AUC(metab) = 3.4 uM x 2 h/0.693 = 9.8 uM.h per dose. Because CCNU/OH-CCNU is chemically unstable (alkylates within hours), NCI-60 'IC50 x 48 h' overstates in-vitro AUC. Two bounds: (i) naive time-average (0.2 uM over 48 h vs IC50 8-27 uM, 2-d assay): k = ln(1+0.2/12)/2 = 0.008/d (range 0.004-0.012); (ii) AUC-matched (in-vitro AUC50 ~ IC50 x t1/2,medium/0.693; t1/2 ~1.5 h gives AUC50 ~26 uM.h at 12 uM): e-folds per dose = ln(1+9.8/26) = 0.32 (range 0.15-0.6) -> ~0.3 e-fold per 21-d cycle = 0.015/d averaged. Dog dose (60-70 mg/m2, ~half human) halves these. So DERIVED k ~0.004-0.04/d, central ~0.015, vs ledger ASSUMED 0.120: the ASSUMED value is 3-8x above what exposure x potency supports, but OUTCOME (RR 87%, CR 50%, TTP 42 d) shows real clinical kill of bulk. Both are consistent: bulk burden falls ~1-2 logs in 3 wk at response, then regrows, which the low derived k does not capture. Report both; do not report 0.12 as DERIVED.
(d) division: alkylation/crosslink damage is cell-cycle NON-specific by chemistry (label: 'cell-cycle non-specific' wording not in label I read; mechanism statement). Evidence on quiescent haematopoietic cells: BCNU/CY minimal effect on late repopulating HSC (PMID 7917905, mouse, REC) => nitrosoureas do NOT reliably kill dormant primitive cells. Grade for dormancy: negative-to-unknown.
(e) pumps: lomustine NOT an ABCB1 substrate: CRISPR ABCB1 knock-out left lomustine response unchanged, whereas temozolomide and carmustine responded (PMID 36252775, GBM cells) [NEW]; P388 vs P388/ADR 1.1x [REC]. BCRP: not found. (Hyokai 33518631 REC: canine lymphoma become P-gp/BCRP-positive after chemo incl. at BBB capillaries.)
(f) CNS: label: 'levels of radioactivity in the CSF are 50% or greater than those measured concurrently in plasma' (Gleostine 2014 label, FDA accessdata 017588s040; read) [NEW exact quote; radioactivity = all metabolites, not parent]. Dog brain tumour outcome proxy: presumptive intracranial glioma n=40, lomustine MST 138 d vs 35 d symptomatic (PMID 29797768) [NEW]. Dog CSF concentration: NOT FOUND. (chlorambucil CSF 0.1 ng/mL, PMID 29775768 in record: do not transfer.) => access factor 0.5 = TRANSFER (human label), supported by a dog brain-tumour outcome.
(g) dCK: independent (alkylator).
(h) escapes: MGMT (canine: 26130852; 29061931; E13 in the ledger), MMR loss, ABCB1 no. 
(i) toxicity. Dog: hepatotoxicity 11/179 (6.1%), delayed, cumulative (median 4 doses/350 mg/m2 vs 3/230), irreversible and fatal in 7 (PMID 14765735); 206-dog series: neutropenia 56.9%, thrombocytopenia 14.2%, ALT up 48.8%, hepatic failure 1.2%, vomiting 24% (PMID 21418164) [NEW]; metronomic 2.84 mg/m2/d: 22/81 stopped for toxicosis (GI 13, thrombocytopenia 4, ALT 3) (PMID 21314727). Human label: delayed myelosuppression 4-6 wk, cumulative; pulmonary >1100 mg/m2. Budget axes: marrow (delayed, cumulative) and hepatic (cumulative, irreversible) agree with ledger (marrow 0.60, hepatic 0.50).
GRADE: potency DERIVED-low-confidence (0.004-0.04/d) + OUTCOME (bulk); brain access TRANSFER 0.5 (+dog outcome proxy); pump: not a substrate (TRANSFER, one KO study); division: cell-cycle-non-specific by mechanism, but dormant kill NOT shown and mouse HSC data negative. Cannot close E5; contributes bulk/brain-bulk.
