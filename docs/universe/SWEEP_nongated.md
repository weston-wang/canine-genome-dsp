## Findings (appended progressively below)

### A. DNA alkylators (class that is not division-gated by chemistry; two of them have CNS data)
**Thiotepa** [REC for brain access/outcome: SWEEP_cnsregimens s8-9; NEW: potency, P-gp evidence, kill derivation]
- IC50 vs lymphoid lines (NEW): NCI-60 48 h GI50 (ChEMBL doc CHEMBL1201862, thiotepa NSC 6396): MOLT-4 7.69 uM, SR (ALCL line) 6.28 uM, CCRF-CEM 9.46 uM, HL-60 7.45 uM. K562 143 uM and RPMI-8226 176 uM are resistant (so spread 20x across lines).
- Pump status (NEW, answers the "NOT FOUND" in SWEEP_cnsregimens): same NCI-60 set has P388 6.93 uM vs P388/ADR (P-gp-overexpressing) 7.41 uM = 1.07-fold, i.e. NO P-gp cross-resistance. Parent drug not shown as P-gp/BCRP substrate in any retrieved record; the glutathione conjugate (monoglutathionylthiotepa) is effluxed by an MRP-type organic-anion pump and GST-P1 raises it (PMID 9788613, MCF-7) = a GSH/MRP escape, not P-gp. Tepadina label (DailyMed 559e353c...) has no transporter statement. Grade: TRANSFER (cell-line pair, 1 drug pair), flag NCI-60 is 48 h, curated PubChem data.
- Human PK (verified): 300 mg/m2 x 3 d, 2 h infusion, 10 children: CL 11.25 L/h/m2, t1/2 1.3 h, Cmax 5.5-25.2 mg/L (PMID 1525606); pop-PK adults CL 34 L/h, V 47 L (PMID 11167666); CSF AUC ratio ~1 (PMID 2491958, n=1) [REC]. Protein binding <=20% [REC].
- Derived kill (my arithmetic): IELSG32/MATRix-type schedule 5 mg/kg x4 doses (about 185 mg/m2 each) AUC = 4 x 185/11.25 = 66 mg.h/L = 349 uM.h parent (MW 189.2), mean over 48 h = 7.3 uM, fu 0.8-1 (fu_medium ~1). With IC50 7-9 uM, 2-day assay: k = ln(1+7.3/8)/2 = 0.33 /day for the 2-3 day course (range 0.1 for IC50 25 uM to 0.5 for 4 uM), TEPA (active, similar alkylating activity, AUC of same order) not added. That is ~1 e-fold per course = the same order as the record's OUTCOME 0.13-0.21 /day program average. So the in-vitro-derived and outcome-derived numbers agree within 2x. This is a PULSE (3 d), not a sustained kill; cannot be repeated without stem-cell rescue.
- Kills non-dividing cells? Mechanism-level only for thiotepa (alkylator crosslink, cell-cycle non-specific; NO direct lymphoma-quiescence citation found). Nearest measured: busulfan, a different alkylator, depletes non-cycling primitive HSC (PMID 11034090 and 7917905, mouse) - but see busulfan below, the property is NOT shared by melphalan/BCNU/CY (7917905).
- Grade for E5-brain: DERIVED/TRANSFER potency + OUTCOME (human CNS lymphoma 3-y PFS 78%) ; dormant-cell kill = assumed from class + outcome plateau, NOT shown.
**Busulfan** [NEW as an E5 candidate]
- Only standard chemotherapy shown to deplete NON-CYCLING primitive stem cells (PMID 11034090, Cancer Res 2000 abstract: "relatively unique among other standard chemotherapy compounds in its ability to deplete noncycling primitive stem cells"); busulfan/dimethylbusulfan especially deplete late (long-term) CAFC while melphalan hits early subsets and CY/BCNU have minimal effect (PMID 7917905, mouse). This is the closest published analogue of E5 (dormant, efflux-high progenitor) that a licensed drug demonstrably kills.
- BUT: lymphoid potency is poor. NCI-60 48 h GI50 CCRF-CEM 145 uM, MOLT-4 124 uM, K562 201 uM (ChEMBL CHEMBL1201862). Human IV/oral busulfan 1 mg/kg q6h x4 d: mean Css 1080 ng/mL = 4.4 uM, "insignificant protein binding", CSF:plasma 1.3 (PMID 2753072, adults, AML). Derived k = ln(1+4.4/130)/2 = 0.017 /day (below the 0.06 bar). DOG: 20 mg/kg IV is marrow-ablative with "a relative sparing of lymphocytes" and 40 mg/kg gave severe CNS toxicity (PMID 10534062). Verdict: brain access good (CSF 1.3) but lymphoid kill rate far below bar. Do not model as an E5 closer. (Does not contradict the HSC finding: HSC are far more busulfan-sensitive than lymphoid lines.)
**Treosulfan** [NEW; not in record]
- Grafapex label (DailyMed 2d06b03c..., US-approved 2025): AUC 1,200 +/- 211 ug.h/mL per dose, not albumin-bound, t1/2 1.7 h; prodrug converted to monoepoxide (EBDM) and diepoxide. Rat CNS (PMID 26428246): unbound brain/plasma AUC 0.07-0.17 (treosulfan), 0.22-0.52 (EBDM); CSF/plasma 0.11-0.12 (treosulfan), 0.64-0.66 (EBDM). In vitro stem cells most sensitive, then NK, T cells (PMID 18246351, pediatric leukemic cells; numbers not in abstract). A glioblastoma trial was negative (PMID 25919318 abstract). No CNS-lymphoma data (PubMed 0). Lymphoma-line IC50 values not retrieved (leukemia lines 0.73-608 uM range over 24-96 h, PMID 16917211). Verdict: potency not derivable from retrieved data; CNS access poorer than thiotepa; NOT ranked.

CORRECTION to thiotepa arithmetic above (script calc_ng.py): using both children CL 11.25 L/h/m2 (PMID 1525606) and adult CL 34 L/h (PMID 11167666), 2 or 4 doses of 5 mg/kg (~194 mg/m2; record SWEEP_cnsregimens s9c schedule, number of doses ambiguous there), mean total conc 3.0-7.6 uM over 36-48 h, fu 0.9, IC50 6.3-9.5 uM: k = 0.13-0.37 /day, central ~0.22 /day, for a 2-3 day pulse (~0.5-0.9 e-fold). Brain access ~1.0 (CSF:plasma AUC 1.01, n=1, PMID 2491958 [REC]). Parent-only; TEPA (similar alkylating activity per PMID 38036843 [REC], longer t1/2 4-17 h) would add. Agrees with the record's OUTCOME 0.13-0.21 /day. P388 vs P388/ADR 1.07x is the first pump evidence for thiotepa: the model's conservative "pump substrate" default for thiotepa (LYMPHOMA_UNIVERSE D) is not supported by anything retrieved; MRP/GST-P1 conjugate efflux (PMID 9788613) is the only transporter link.

### C. Antimetabolite-type that kill NON-dividing lymphocytes
**Cladribine** [NEW class; REC says only "none found in dog lymphoma"]: toxic to non-dividing human lymphocytes (PMID 2579098, JCI 1985); crosses BBB (MS literature: PMIDs 32492189, 39663507; CSF 0.1-1 uM "putatively"); BUT ABCG2/BCRP transports cladribine and confers resistance in transfected cells (PMID 18765824; also PMID 17638908) and activation needs dCK (E2). Fails criterion (ii) for BCRP-high E5 and is defeated by E2 -> EXCLUDED with stated scientific reason (named escapes, not absence of canine data).

### D. Transcription-level (not cell-cycle) killers
**CDK9 inhibitors (AZD4573 = zemirciclib; enitociclib/VIP152; flavopiridol [REC])** [NEW as a class for E5]
- Non-gated: inhibition of cell-cycle CDKs (1, 4/6, 7) is non-cytotoxic; only CDK9 (AZD4573, atuveciclib) and CDK12/13 trigger apoptosis by collapsing short-lived Mcl-1 and A1/Bfl-1 in Jurkat and SU-DHL-1 (PMID 42204137, 2026). A dual Cdc7/Cdk9 inhibitor kills QUIESCENT primary CLL cells via Mcl-1 loss (PMID 21768328). AZD4573 or enitociclib overcomes Bfl-1-mediated BH3-mimetic resistance in lymphoma lines (PMID 33259592) and BTKi/CAR-T resistance in MCL (PMID 38886769, enitociclib in MCL; abstract-level).
- Potency (ChEMBL, human): AZD4573 MINO (MCL) IC50 22 nM, 4 d (CHEMBL5500401); OPM-2 GI50 23 nM 24 h; MV4-11 caspase EC50 13.7 nM at 6 h. Canine: flavopiridol kills all 8 canine lines at 400 nM [REC, PMID 25623777]; canine AZD4573/enitociclib: none.
- Human exposure/clinical: enitociclib 30 mg IV weekly (MTD) Cmax 836 ug/L (~2 uM), AUC 2,780 ug.h/L (search-snippet of PMID 35046056 full text; abstract itself confirms MTD 30 mg weekly, 2/7 HGL durable complete metabolic remissions ongoing at 3.7 and 2.3 years on treatment, grade 3/4 neutropenia 22%). AZD4573 12 mg weekly RP2D, t1/2 ~6 h, PTCL early analysis 3/12 CR lasting 7.7 to 17.4+ wk (conference abstracts via web search; NOT peer-reviewed, not fetched in full).
- Kill rate: not derivable by the model formula. Pulse of hours: time-average gives k 0.06-0.15 /day, peak gives 0.7-0.9 /day for hours; the hinge is hours above IC50 vs Mcl-1/Bfl-1 resynthesis. Grade: TRANSFER-OUTCOME, kill rate ASSUMED.
- P-gp/BCRP status: NOT FOUND for enitociclib or AZD4573 (searched). CNS/CSF penetration: NOT FOUND. No CNS-lymphoma or dog data (flavopiridol only in vitro/xenograft). Toxicity: neutropenia, GI; arrhythmia-QT not checked.
- Verdict: best mechanistic fit to "kills non-cycling, not cell-cycle" but criteria (ii) and (iii) are UNVERIFIED; this is the highest-value measurement to make next (brain/CSF Kp,uu of enitociclib in a rodent; Abcb1/Abcg2 transport assay).

### E. Mitochondrial/metabolic and cell-death modulators
- **IACS-010759** (complex I): phase I AML (n=17) + solid tumours (n=23): dose-limiting elevated blood lactate and NEUROTOXICITY, no RP2D, modest target inhibition, limited activity, both trials discontinued (Nat Med 2023, PMID 36658425). EXCLUDED by toxicity (stated scientific reason; brain is the compartment at risk).
- **ONC201 / dordaviprone** [NEW; FDA-approved 2025 for H3K27M glioma, label DailyMed ad45b43e...]: oral weekly; Cmax 2.8 ug/mL (7.2 uM), AUC 23 ug.h/mL, t1/2 11 h, protein binding 95-97%, Vd 450 L; label states no clinically significant interaction predicted with P-gp/BCRP substrates but gives NO substrate statement. Crosses intact BBB (PMID 33142238 abstract). Lymphoma lines: Z-138 (MCL) IC50 1.0 uM 72 h, Raji 2.48 uM 48 h (ChEMBL CHEMBL5223078, CHEMBL5154926). Derived k: time-average 0.006-0.017 /day (fails the 0.06 bar); only the 7 uM peak (hours) would exceed IC50. EXCLUDED on potency.
- **Arsenic trioxide** [NEW]: DLBCL-line IC50 2.21 uM (PMID 41825143, CCK-8; organic arsenicals 0.67-1.1 uM, PMID 41825143, 42152103). CSF arsenic is only ~14-19% of plasma (CSF 95.8 vs plasma 498.9 nmol/L median, oral ATO, PMID 18703707; CSF 0.11 vs ~0.8 uM, PMID 17416415) -> CSF 0.1 uM << 2.2 uM IC50: k = 0.014-0.024 /day. MRP1 transports As-GSH (PMID 32470611). Veterinary-licensed organic arsenical melarsomine (heartworm) suppresses canine OSA lines via Hedgehog-GLI (PMID 31645504); canine lymphoma: NOT FOUND. EXCLUDED on CNS access (brain).
- **Artesunate** [REC: only that it was a hit in the 1,824-compound screen, IC50 not extracted; NEW: numbers]: canine GL-1/UL-1/CLBL-1 IC50 0.22/0.33/0.54 uM at 72 h; dog Cmax 3,560 ng/mL (9.27 uM) IV, 1,149 ng/mL (2.99 uM) IM (PMC12495782 Table 2 of PMID 41044621; dog IM PK PMID 18606761). Human B-cell lines: IC50 comparable to malaria-patient serum, normal B cells spared, ER-stress/UPR + metabolic suppression (PMID 29458389); ferroptosis via PRDX1/2 in DLBCL (PMID 40645965). "ART maintains a high concentration in the brain" (rat, abstract sentence, PMID 33390425, used in cerebral malaria). Derived (my arithmetic; AS t1/2 0.25 h and DHA AUC = 2x AS ASSUMED, fu assumed 0.1-0.5): daily dosing mean 0.42 uM total -> k 0.05-0.24 /day, central ~0.1. Non-gated: NOT SHOWN (G1 arrest in PEL, PMID 32885355). P-gp/BCRP: NOT FOUND. Human lymphoma trial: NONE. Grade: DERIVED-lite (canine IC50 x dog Cmax; PK/binding assumptions) -> best "licensed + canine-IC50" non-pump candidate but exposure model weak.
- **Ferroptosis inducers (GPX4 inhibitors), cardiac glycosides, SMAC mimetics, HSP90**: no clinical-stage agent with lymphoma-line IC50 + exposure retrieved. Ouabain kills Raji (PMID 27894666) but cardiac toxicity in dogs not checked; tolinapant (oral IAP antagonist) has phase 1 PTCL/CTCL single-agent activity (PMID 31900279, 34474469) but no CNS data. Hold as "no usable number".

### F. Immunomodulatory / targeted classes the user listed (what is new vs record)
- **BCL6**: PREMISE CORRECTION (NEW). Canine B-cell lymphoma "rarely expressed BCL6 and MUM1/IRF4 by IHC" and is ABC-like NF-kB-driven (Richards 2013, PMID 23783577); review: "absence of BCL6 expression" in canine DLBCL (PMID 32038991); BCL6 mRNA not prognostic in 44 dogs (PMID 21277811). Canine BCL6 cDNA is 96.3% identical, POZ domain identical (PMID 21216477), so a degrader would bind, but there is no canine dependency evidence. Human degraders ARV-393 (PROTAC) and BMS-986458 (cereblon-based, inherits the canine CRBN question [REC]) are in phase 1 (ASCO 2026 TPS7103); no brain data found. Not a canine E5 closer on current evidence.
- **Lenalidomide/pomalidomide** [REC]: canine CRBN inference stands; no new canine data (PubMed/web re-searched, nothing); direct kill 0.0004-0.025 /day [REC].
- **CD47 blockade** [REC E10]: NEW fact: magrolimab was discontinued in heme malignancies after futility and increased risk of death (infections, respiratory failure) in ENHANCE, ENHANCE-2/3 (web: Gilead 2023-24); antibody CNS access poor [REC]. Not a brain closer.
- **Alemtuzumab/anti-CD52** [REC excluded]. **Mogamulizumab/anti-CCR4**: NEW: no canine CCR4 antibody or trial found; CCR4 only as skin-homing transcript in canine epitheliotropic lymphoma (PMID 21945178, 24118305). **Brentuximab/anti-CD30** [REC: no canine therapeutic CD30 antibody].
- **NK products / CAR-NK**: cord-blood CAR19/IL-15 NK, 37 patients, ORR 48.6% day 30 and 100, 1-y PFS 32%, no CRS, no neurotoxicity, no GVHD (PMID 38238616); other CAR-NK PMIDs 32023374, 40251398. Canine expanded NK safe in dogs (solid tumours; PMIDs 38631708, 42461253 [REC]). Quiescent-cell argument (NEW, mechanism only): quiescent stem cells down-regulate MHC-I/TAP via NLRC5 and evade activated T cells (PMID 29466757, mouse epithelial), which weakens MHC-I-restricted effectors (vaccines, GVL) against E5 and favours MHC-independent killers (antibody, CAR, engagers, NK); NK control of dormant stem-like cells shown in mouse breast cancer (PMID 39106452). No lymphoma-quiescence or CNS data for NK. Grade: ASSUMED kill, TRANSFER safety.
- **Radioimmunotherapy**: 111In/90Y-ibritumomab IV in recurrent PCNSL, n=6: median absorbed dose 701 cGy to lymphoma vs 70 cGy to normal brain; median PFS 6.8 wk, OS 14.3 wk (PMID 17932895). Radiation is not gated and not pumped but the dose and duration are inadequate; intraventricular 131I-antibody phase 1s exist for solid tumours (PMIDs 18048828, 9816257), none for lymphoma. EXCLUDED as a closer on dose/duration.
- **PI3Kd, BTKi, EZH2, ADCs, proteasome, HDAC, CDK4/6, venetoclax**: all [REC] as pump substrates, gated, or sub-bar. NEW additions: PBD payload (loncastuximab SG3199) acquired resistance involves ABCG2 and ABCC2, not ABCB1 (PMID 32669316) -> BCRP-high E5 defeats it, and canine CD19 antibody 4E9 does not cross-react with human CD19 [REC]. 
- **PRMT5 / menin / SYK / JAK / BET / SMAC / HSP90**: PRMT5 needs MTAP loss; canine MTAP/CDKN2A co-deletion: NOT FOUND (PubMed 0). Menin inhibitors are KMT2A/NPM1 leukaemia drugs (not lymphoma). SYK, JAK (ruxolitinib; oclacitinib), BET [REC]. None meets criteria (i)-(iii) with a number.
