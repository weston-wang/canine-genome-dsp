## Human GBM surgical-window (Lassman 2022, Clin Cancer Res, PMID 34728525, PMC8810630, full text read) -- already in record for the 105.4 nM figure; NEW: the ratio
- Arm A n=8, 50 mg/m2 BIW up to 3 doses, last dose 2-24 h pre-op. Tumour (contrast-ENHANCING, BBB disrupted) 39.7-291 nM, median 105.4; plasma "2 hours post-dosing" 645 nM-1.62 uM, median 835 nM; **tumour/plasma 0.0616-0.190, median 0.0914** (Suppl Table S4). - Efficacy in GBM (non-surgical arms): PFS6 10%/7.7%/17%, RANO RR 8.8%; 80 mg weekly best; dose reductions 28.9%; AEs fatigue 61%, nausea 59%, anorexia 43%, thrombocytopenia 43%; arm B stopped for fatigue/anorexia/thrombocytopenia.

## Canine verdinexor (KPT-335) numbers re-read from PMC full text (PMC3913620 = London 2014 PMID 24503695; PMC6109271 = Sadowski 2018 PMID 30143046)
- Healthy dog PK 1.5 mg/kg PO fed: Cmax 253 ng/mL, Tmax 3.8 h, t1/2 3.9 h, AUC0-inf 1810 h.ng/mL (24503695 Table 2). Phase II dogs (1.25-1.5 mg/kg MWF): Cmax 278, AUC 1971, t1/2 ~5 h (30143046).
- Phase I MTD 1.75 mg/kg BIW; DLTs hepatotoxicity, anorexia, weight loss; canine NHL line IC50 2-42 nM (24503695; already in record SWEEP_modalities).
- Phase II (n=58; 54 evaluable): ORR 37% (20/54); T-cell ORR 71% (abstract; n not stated in what I read; 7 naive + 7 relapsed T-cell dogs enrolled); overall median DOR 18 d (7-152), median TTP 29 d; naive T-cell TTP 31 d; first-relapse T-cell TTP 43 d (n=7); TTP 73 d if prednisone given on/after day 0. EXCLUSION: "no evidence of central nervous system involvement" (so NO CNS efficacy/exposure data). Phase I also excluded brain mets.
- Neither paper reports a tissue distribution or CNS neuro AE in the text I grepped (no tremor/seizure hits in PMC6109271).
## FDA Xpovio (selinexor) NDA 212306 multidisciplinary review (downloaded, text extracted; accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/212306Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf) -- NEW
- Rat QWBA, single 5 mg/kg PO [14C]-selinexor, to 168 h: highest in kidney, liver, small intestine; "Distribution to the brain was observed" (qualitative only, no brain:plasma number in review). ## Pediatric selinexor trial ADVL1414 (Green 2025 Clin Cancer Res, PMID 39998852, PMC12045713) -- NEW, CNS exposure by clinical effect
- Abstract calls selinexor "CNS-penetrant". DLTs on BIW 3/1 (20 mg/m2): fatigue, "acute reversible neurologic changes" (one grade 3 reversible cerebellar dysfunction); on weekly (45 mg/m2): grade 3 seizure. Interim PK suggested Cmax at BIW MTD "may not be sufficient to achieve adequate CNS concentrations" -> switched to weekly. 35 mg/m2 QW: Cmax 599 ng/mL, AUC0-24 4885 h.ng/mL, t1/2 7.5 h. No objective responses (n=59; 10 HGG expansion); only 8/59 got 5-9 cycles. Text: dexamethasone prophylaxis "has the potential to decrease selinexor's BBB penetration" (cites mouse dex-BBB paper PMID 3707089). Authors state need "to understand differences in brain penetration" (no brain measurement in this trial).
