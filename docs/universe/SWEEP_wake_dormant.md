## (1) CXCR4 antagonists — verified (abstract or PMC text read)
- 28282031 (Uy 2017 BCJ, Ph1/2 plerixafor+G-CSF+MEC, R/R AML, n=35): phase II CR/CRi 6/20 = 30% < 35% threshold; terminated for futility; blasts mobilised 3.5-fold (leukocytes 2.4x) after 2 d G-CSF; Ki67 only "slight but highly variable" increase; authors: G-CSF prosurvival signals counteract priming/pro-apoptotic effects. NEGATIVE for priming.
- 33270904 (Borthakur 2021 Cancer, BL-8040 [motixafortide] + HiDAC, R/R AML Ph2a n=42): composite CR 29% (12/42), 39% (9/23) at 1.5 mg/kg; mOS 8.4 mo (10.8 at 1.5); 2 d BL-8040 monotherapy mobilised blasts, more in responders. Single-arm.
- 32697348 (Ph1 sorafenib+G-CSF+plerixafor FLT3-ITD AML n=28): ORR 36%; blast mobilisation 58.4-fold, CD34+38- 47-fold.
- 28409853 (POE 10-03, plerixafor+HiDAC+etoposide, paeds n=19): CR 2 + CRi 1 (all AML), 0 in ALL; blast mobilisation median 3.4-fold, correlated with surface CXCR4.
- 25867648 (Ph1/2 G-CSF+plerixafor + Bu-Flu allo, n=45): no RFS/OS difference vs 164 historical; preferentially mobilised FISH+ leukaemic cells. 30654137 (plerixafor + MA conditioning n=12): safe; 2/12 relapsed.
- 31112940: CXCR4 inhibition mobilised AML cells "associated with molecular markers of cell cycle progression" (RPPA; n=33; association only).
- 22509798 (Lee 2012 BJH, FL cancer stem cells): SP (ABCG2+) FLK-1 cells enriched by chemo/RT, adhere to FDC via CXCL12/CXCR4, need FDC to form tumours in NOD/SCID; AMD3100 "eliminated tumour growth". Mouse/in vitro; confirmed. Lymphoma niche precedent; no kill rate.
- 31352850 (Ph1 plerixafor+rituximab R/R CLL n=24, NCT00694590): MTD 320 ug/kg; CLL cells mobilised median 3.3-fold (1.2-12.4); ORR 38%, correlated with plerixafor dose; no TLS. (Rituximab alone ORR in R/R CLL not given in abstract -> no control.)
- 25333254 (Oncotarget 2014): plerixafor + chemo reduces leukaemic burden vs chemo alone in pALL xenografts; prolonged plerixafor -> CXCR4 up-regulation (escape). Preclinical. 28409853: no ALL responses in humans.
- 34050566 review: "drug-resistant leukaemia cells were not affected by CXCR4 antagonists", niche migration can be CXCR4-independent.
## (5) CNS niche

## (2) G-CSF / GM-CSF / IL-3 priming (AML evidence; all verified via abstract)
- 1961040 (GM-CSF 8 ug/kg/d CIV 72 h, n=7 AML/CML-BC): marrow blast S-phase labelling index up in 5/7, fold 1.3/1.5/1.9/2.3/3.2 (RNA index also up = quiescent->cycle); preferential BrdU+ cell kill with chemo in 5/7 CR pts. => MEASURED in humans: awake fraction x1.3-3.2 in 5 of 7 (small n, 1991, myeloid).
- 7577641 (IL-3 250 ug/m2/d 6-10 d, n=3 monosomy-7 AML): recruited blasts in 2/3, normal cells not recruited; in vitro araC cytotoxicity up; "other mechanisms of primary resistance may limit benefit".
- 22422824 (HOVON-42, n=917 randomised G-CSF vs none): no benefit overall (5-y OS 43 vs 40%, relapse 34 vs 36%); benefit only with escalated-dose cytarabine (EFS P=.01, OS P=.003). 22111065 (n=34): no benefit. 17252021 (ALFA GM-CSF, n=259): CR 88 vs 78% (P<0.04), 3-y EFS 42 vs 34%, OS no change. 38755516 (JSCO 2024 meta, 10 trials): relapse RR 0.91 (0.82-1.01), p=0.08; OS benefit only intermediate-risk and HiDAC subgroups. => net: modest, not a step change (RR relapse ~0.9).
- 30208330: G-CSF up-regulates miR-146a, lowers CXCR4 and Smad4, promotes cell-cycle entry and araC apoptosis in vitro; high/up-regulated miR-146a predicted outcome in 78 AML (association).
## (3) ibrutinib / niche exit precedent
- 37717577 (2024 review): BTK/PI3K inhibitors mobilise CLL cells from tissue to blood = "transient redistribution lymphocytosis", depriving cells of niche factors (class effect, mechanism review).
- 25797245 (Oncotarget 2015, MCL): resistance to venetoclax from CD40 stroma lost on detachment from CD40L fibroblasts; in 2 ibrutinib-treated patients mobilised MCL cells highly sensitive to ABT-199 (n=2). Precedent for 'niche exit -> drug sensitive'.
- 27480113: ibrutinib/plerixafor block CXCL12-driven CD20 up-regulation in CLL; recently-egressed CXCR4-dim cells have higher CD20.
- Canine G-CSF in lymphoma: PubMed 'G-CSF dogs lymphoma' = no priming study (hits are vaccine/cytokine papers); canine hGM-CSF vaccine trials null (19754780, 21569195; already in record).

## (4) Switching/division rate data
- 19062086 (Wilson 2008 Cell): mouse dormant HSC divide every ~145 d (5x/lifetime) by BrdU/H2B-GFP + modelling; d-HSCs "efficiently activated to self-renew in response to bone marrow injury or G-CSF stimulation", then return to dormancy. => MEASURED (mouse HSC): wake by G-CSF; baseline dormant->active ~ 1/145 = 0.007/day.
- 21343613 (Catlin 2011 Blood): human HSC replicate once per 40 weeks (range 25-50 wk) = 0.0036/day (0.0029-0.0057), X-inactivation inference.
- 27839867 (Bernitz 2016 Cell): slow-cycling mouse HSC: 4 traceable divisions then dormancy; division intervals lengthen.
- 19212321 (Essers 2009 Nature): mouse IFN-alpha drives dormant HSC out of G0; IFN-primed HSC "efficiently eliminated" by 5-FU, unprimed dormant resistant. => MEASURED in mouse: priming converts a drug-insensitive dormant pool to a killed one.
- 27916615 (Ebinger 2016 Cancer Cell, PDX ALL): rare dormant, treatment-resistant, stem-like cells resemble MRD; "resistant, dormant cells became sensitive to treatment and started proliferating when dissociated from the in vivo environment"; authors: strategies that release MRD cells from the niche. => MEASURED (xenograft, lymphoid): dormancy is niche-reversible. Direct lymphoid support for the strategy.
- 20371479 (Saito 2010 STM): AML LSC CD32/CD25+ are cell-cycle quiescent and chemo-resistant in vivo (53% pts).
## More (1)/(3) verified

- 41980027 (Blood 2026, NCT02502968, RANDOMISED double-blind Ph2, n=128, AML first remission, HiDAC +/- motixafortide): median RFS 10.3 mo (8.0-12.0) vs 11.5 mo (8.6-24.1), log-rank P=.98 = NULL overall. Subgroup: CXCR4-high pts benefit with motixafortide (P=.047), CXCR4-high do worse on placebo (P=.02). => Strongest evidence on record: unselected CXCR4 chemosensitisation does not work in AML consolidation; biomarker-selected might.
