## Verified so far
- Golidocitinib JACKPOT8 Part B ph2 (PMID 38092009, Lancet Oncol 2023;25:117): n=104 dosed, 88 evaluable, 150 mg QD oral, ORR 44.3% (CR 24%), gr3-4 neutropenia 29%; Part A ph1 (PMID 37673210): ORR 39.2%, median DoR 8.0 mo, PFS 3.3 mo. CNS data: NOT in these abstracts (to check).
- CNS T/NK review (PMID 30919085): HD-MTX-based regimens most effective, long-term survival only in a few reports; CTCL with CNS involvement n=4 (PMID 37877303): survival after CNS metastasis ~160 d median, none >5 y.
- Golidocitinib (Dizal; China conditional approval Jun 2024 per Drugs first-approval summary via web search; FDA Fast Track): CNS/CSF/brain data NOT FOUND (PubMed 0 hits for CNS; EPMC no primary data). Dizal's brain-penetrant compound is a different one (DZD8586 LYN/BTK, B-NHL).
- Ruxolitinib CSF (human): PMID 42138216 (J Cell Mol Med 2026, doi 10.1111/jcmm.71173), 10 sHLH pts, BBB penetrability 5.31-18.08% (abstract only; CSF:plasma total). Rux fu 0.03 (record) -> CSF/total 5-18% exceeds free fraction, i.e. no evidence of efflux exclusion; rux is not P-gp substrate (label, record). NEW (CSF human value).
- Soquelitinib (Corvus; covalent ITK inhibitor): ph3 registrational PTCL (150 pts vs belinostat/pralatrexate, PFS primary endpoint), FDA ODD+Fast Track; interim late 2026 (web: Corvus Q2-2026 release; SEC 8-K). No CNS data. Not in PubMed under name.
- **NEW (grep zero in repo): CPI-818 = soquelitinib in DOGS with spontaneous T-cell lymphoma.** Thamm (Flint Animal Cancer Center, CSU) / Hill (Corvus), Eur J Cancer 2018;103:E5; Buggy 2019 Hematol Oncol (doi 10.1002/hon.129_2630): 20 mg/kg PO BID, 2 wk-5 mo, n=3 (1 PTCL, 2 CTCL): 1 CR (CTCL, 5 mo) + 2 PR; "no change in normal lymphocyte counts"; well tolerated (web search snippet of Corvus/Wiley pages; abstract-level; n=3). Also counted in Front Vet Sci 2026 TKI meta-analysis (PMID 42656497; doi 10.3389/fvets.2026.1852091; 101 dogs, 11 trials, ORR T-cell 0.63 (25/39) vs B 0.27; trials: masitinib (Holtermann PMID 26364581 7/10), toceranib 2/2 MDR T, CPI-818 2/3 [meta counts PR+CR], oclacitinib 1/2 T (Baba PMID 40525607)). Single-arm, tiny n.
- Soquelitinib human ph1 (Blood 2026, PMID 42640855, doi 10.1182/blood.2026034704): n=75 R/R PTCL, no DLT to 600 mg BID, target occupancy >80% at 200 mg BID, durable CR/PR in 37% of pts with 1-3 prior lines, none in >3 lines; mechanism Th1 skewing/ITK, effects "on malignant T cells and/or normal T effector cells" (i.e. may be immune-mediated, not direct kill). Ph3 (150 pts vs belinostat/pralatrexate) enrolling; interim late 2026.
- Duvelisib+romidepsin ph1b/2a (PMID 38886623, Nat Med 2024): TCL ORR 55% CR 34% (n=64); PTCL 56%/44% (n=48); duvelisib mono hepatotox gr3/4 40%. No CNS data. Already in record: duvelisib P-gp/BCRP substrate; dog RV1001 TTP 21-25 d.
- Linperlisib (China PI3Kd): PTCL data; linperlisib+chidamide ph2 (Leuk Lymphoma 2026 PMID 41773975?; JAMA Dermatol 2025 CTCL) - no CNS data found.
- Human CNS T-cell lymphoma outcomes (all NEW to record; grep 'CNS T-cell' only in STATUS): PCNSTL multicentre n=16 (Munich/Heidelberg/MGH; Neurology 2026, PMID 41843861): median PFS 4 mo, median OS 97.5 mo; MTX-based chemo + ASCT best outcome (CI 2-87 mo, p=0.0275). Systematic case review n=136 PCNSTL (BMJ Neurol Open 2026, PMID 41509524): ALCL meningeal ~50%; 7/14 CSF flow positive. Czech registry (PMID 35646641, Front Oncol 2022): 29/1040 PTCL (2.79%) with CNS involvement; median PFS 4.8 mo (initial CNS+systemic) vs 32.6; median OS 18.2 vs 46.0 mo; OS after CNS relapse 11.8 mo; 44.8% got HD-MTX. North American CITI (PMID 38739715, Blood Adv 2024): 135 MTNKN CNS relapses; median time to CNS relapse 8.0 mo, median OS after CNS relapse 4.7 mo; 10-yr CNS relapse risk 2.2% low-risk vs 13.4% high-risk. CNS-involved CTCL n=4 (PMID 37877303): median survival after CNS 160 d. Review (PMID 30919085): HD-MTX regimens best; long-term survival only in a few reports. => Human data: CNS T-cell disease is lethal in months with current agents; only HD-MTX(+ASCT) shows tails.
- **NEW: mogamulizumab (human anti-CCR4 mAb) was given to dogs**: JITC 2022 (PMID 35131860, doi 10.1136/jitc-2021-003731; Japan group), non-randomised canine trial in advanced prostate cancer: circulating Tregs fell, improved survival, few AEs => the human mAb binds canine CCR4 and is tolerated in dogs. Record (SWEEP_nongated l.58) said "no canine CCR4 antibody found" - this refines it: reagent exists/used in dogs (not tested in canine lymphoma). Mogamulizumab is licensed in humans for CTCL (MF/SS); ADCC-based, so effector-cell dependent, not pumped; IgG CSF access ~0.1-0.5% (class value, not verified here).
- Allogeneic canine NK ph1 now peer-reviewed (Vet Comp Oncol 2026, PMID 42461253, doi 10.1111/vco.70091): 3 dogs, metastatic solid tumours (osteosarcoma line potency), lymphodepleted, tolerated. NOT lymphoma; in record as preprint (3 dogs).
- Human CNS relapse in T-cell lymphoma, 4 cases (Leuk Res Rep 2025, PMID 40672809): median survival 1.5-3.5 mo despite IT HD-MTX, salvage chemo, immunotherapy.
- Valemetostat (EZH1/2; Japan-approved PTCL): VALENTINE-PTCL01 ph2 (PMID 39486433, Lancet Oncol 2024): oral 200 mg/day continuous, ORR 44% (52/119), gr3-4 thrombocytopenia 23%; plasma protein binding >94%, CYP3A (PMID 40208327). CNS/CSF and P-gp: NOT FOUND. Record has tazemetostat only (P-gp substrate), not valemetostat -> NEW but no CNS evidence.
- Nelarabine (licensed T-ALL/T-LBL): 'excellent BBB penetration' per case series (PMID 39013740, 135 pts, severe neurotoxicity 4% (5/135), irreversible mostly); mode-of-action review PMID 39962329 (ara-G -> ara-GTP; nuclear DNA synthesis target = division-dependent; activation via dCK/dGK so partly shares E2 dCK-loss vulnerability: from review abstract only - UNVERIFIED which kinase). No canine data found. Record: nelarabine only in SWEEP_modalities. Division-gated (DNA synthesis) => does not address E5.

## CONCLUSION (strength-labelled)
No agent in this sweep has all of: non-division-gated, non-pump, measured CNS access, measured T-cell kill with canine-transferable exposure, and multi-year continuous safety. Rule 11/14: do not report 'unmeasured in dog' as a gap; the failing quantities are the ones with no basis: (i) CSF/brain level of every non-CAR T-cell agent except ruxolitinib (human 5-18%), venetoclax (0.74%), nelarabine ('excellent', qualitative), (ii) action on the DORMANT T-lineage progenitor: no candidate has any direct evidence (JAK1/ITK/IL-7 survival dependence is a mechanism-level TRANSFER at best).
