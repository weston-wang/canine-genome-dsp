## (1) FOCUSED ULTRASOUND BBB opening
- DOG (only large-animal data found): PMID 28912896 (O'Reilly, Hynynen, Theranostics 2017, DOI 10.7150/thno.20621): 10 aged beagles, low-frequency FUS + microbubbles, whole HEMISPHERE (unilateral) opening, 1 or 4 weekly sessions; MRI Gd-enhancement increase mean 19+/-11% vs untreated hemisphere; 1 adverse event resolved <24 h; microhaemorrhage comparable (single 1.0+/-1.4 vs 0.4+/-0.5; repeat 5.2+/-1.8 vs 4.0+/-2.0); no tissue damage on MRI. NO DRUG CONCENTRATION measured -> fold-increase in dog = NOT FOUND. Status: NEW.
- Clinical canine FUS (Purdue: osteosarcoma only; fusfoundation.org page 403, search snippet) and VT/Wisconsin histotripsy (3 meningioma dogs; ablation, not BBB opening). No canine BBB-opening drug-delivery trial for glioma/lymphoma found on PubMed, FUS Foundation or VT trial pages. Fetch of VT page 'canine-gliomas' lists only a CED trial. => no veterinary centre offers FUS-BBB opening as a clinical service (not found).
- HUMAN: PMID 30674905 (Mainprize, Sci Rep 2019; PMC6344541): 5 glioma pts, 220 kHz, Gd enhancement +15-50%, resolves ~20 h; chemo measurable in only 2 pts: liposomal doxorubicin sonicated 0.22 vs unsonicated 0.15 ng/mg (1.5x); TMZ 3.47e-4 vs 0.45e-4 ng/mg (7.7x; one patient). No adverse events.
- HUMAN SonoCloud-9 (implanted, carboplatin): Nat Commun 2024 (DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-45818-7), Gd enhancement after >90% sonications, 2 gr3 wound infections; Phase 3 SONOBIRD recruiting through 2026 (search snippet; paper itself behind paywall: carboplatin tissue fold NOT verified).
- PRECLINICAL fold increases (search-snippet level, UNVERIFIED primary): trastuzumab 12+/-5-fold at 2 h, none at 24 h (spinal cord/PMC7904756); IgG 5-6-fold (mouse); TMZ CSF/plasma 22.7% -> 38.6%; TMZ brain 6.98 -> 19 ng/mg (2.7x); liposomal dox up to 2369+/-946 ng/g.
- LYMPHOMA-SPECIFIC: FUS for CNS lymphoma = preclinical only (Calimeri review, Ann Lymphoma, aol.amegroups.org/article/view/7321). Window closes <~24 h; antibody delivery works (IgG x5-12) -> would lift anti-CD20 0.002 only for the SONICATED volume.
- LIMITS: FUS opening is volume-limited (hemisphere is the largest dog data; whole-brain not shown); needs MRI guidance + microbubble IV; diffuse leptomeningeal/CSF disease not reached (CSF-facing tissue; drug entering from blood). P-gp substrates remain pumped even when tight junctions open? (not shown; unverified).

## (2) OSMOTIC BBBD
- DOG: PMID 6809316 (Neuwelt 1981 Cancer Treat Rep 65 S2:39): intracarotid 25% mannitol disrupts BBB reproducibly (ICA, not CCA); MTX brain levels significantly higher vs control (abstract gives no fold). PMID 6153461 (Neuwelt 1980 Neurosurgery 6:49): dog; MTX brain levels rise in areas matching CT enhancement. PMID 6774280 already in record. PMID 9588554 (Kroll & Neuwelt 1998 review).
- HUMAN: PMID 19451444 (Angelov JCO 2009; PMC2717756): 149 newly diagnosed PCNSL, osmotic BBBD + intra-arterial MTX, 4 centres 1982-2005: ORR 81.9% (CR 57.8%), median OS 3.1 y (25% alive at 8.5 y), median PFS 1.8 y, 5-y PFS 31%, 7-y PFS 25%; low-risk (age<60, KPS>=70) median OS ~14 y with plateau after ~8 y; focal seizures 9.2%, no long-term sequelae. => REAL DATA durable tail in lymphoma from an access lever. "50-100-fold" increase in diseased brain/CSF per Calimeri review citing refs 40,49 (secondary citation; primary not verified). Relapses in 81% at spatially DISTINCT sites (N=37 series, Calimeri) -> access lift works locally, regional relapse not prevented.
- Not a dog-lymphoma practice. No veterinary use found. Requires angiography, anaesthesia, ICA catheter, 25% mannitol ~ 1-2 min infusions; dog model exists since 1980.

## (3) CED in dogs
- PMID 18447717 (Dickinson, J Neurosurg 2008, DOI 10.3171/JNS/2008/108/5/0989): 4 healthy dogs, CED of Gd/CPT-11 liposomes under real-time MRI; robust Vd in grey+white; variability greatest in grey matter, linked to leakage into ventricles/subarachnoid space; minimal AEs (transient proprioceptive deficits, focal haemorrhage 1, mild encephalitis 1).
- PMID 20488958 (Dickinson, Neuro-Oncology 2010; PMC2940703): 9 dogs spontaneous glioma, 27 infusions, max 2.125 mL/procedure at 0.1-5 uL/min (135-290 min); Vd coverage of tumour 0.02-90% (mean 38%, median 28%); tumour volume (1.3-7.1 cm3) decreased up to 88%, 5/9 >=40%; survival 126-611 d; 2 dogs white-matter oedema (steroid-responsive); main limiter = leak into ventricles/subarachnoid space.
- Young 2018 World Neurosurg (sciencedirect S1878875018313305; no PMID retrieved): PMNP-TMZ CED in 10 dogs, 70% accurately on target, no backflow, safe. Freeman: cetuximab-iron oxide CED 8 dogs MST 248 d (via PMC10643662 review; Frontiers Vet Sci 2023 chemo-for-glioma review).
- ACTIVE: Virginia Tech/Wake Forest NIH-funded 'Molecular Combinatorial Therapy for Canine Malignant Gliomas' CED trial (research.vetmed.vt.edu/clinical-trials/canine-gliomas.html; dogs 3-45 kg, MRI single mass). CSU/CU glioma CAR-T + verdinexor trial is intratumoral (already in record H.1).
- FEASIBILITY for lymphoma: CED is a point-source parenchymal method (<=2 mL per procedure; coverage mean 38% of a 4-cm3 tumour; leakage to CSF spaces). Diffuse leptomeningeal/periventricular/perivascular lymphoma => NOT feasible as whole-brain access; can only treat a focal mass. Grade: dog data real, but it does not lift a diffuse sanctuary. 

## (9) CANINE CNS LYMPHOMA: how treated now / T vs B (record check)
- Already in record: LaRue 30022475 (n=18, MST 171 d, range 1-1942), Sisó 27511313, MRI 22093168/33769594/36329600, 31769013 (cytarabine CRI toxicity n=26 CNS/BM), 36899719.
- NEW: Sisó 2016 finding that PTCL shows PERIPHERAL-NERVE involvement while DLBCL shows meningeal/perivascular/periventricular (27511313 abstract; in record only as a citation?). 
- NEW: PMID 12056776 (McDonough 2002 J Comp Pathol): canine intravascular lymphoma n=17 (CNS-predominant): 8/15 T-cell, 1 B, 6 non-T non-B. PMID 30286705 (Degl'Innocenti 2018): 10 CNS-restricted IVL: 3 T, 3 B, 4 non-T/non-B. => in IVL the tumour cells sit INSIDE vessels: blood-borne drugs and antibodies reach them without crossing the BBB (my inference; not shown in a paper).
- NEW: PMID 29805960 (Treggiari 2018 Open Vet J): LGL T-cell lymphoma with spinal meningeal infiltrate L2-L7, lomustine/cytarabine protocol, rapid neurological improvement, euthanised at relapse day 195 (n=1).
- NEW: Front Vet Sci 2023;10:1209935 (PMC10507905): B-cell lymphoblastic lymphoma stage Vb, 4-y dog; cisternal IT MTX 2.5 mg + cytarabine 100 mg; CSF 1,900 cells/uL; clinical remission within 24 h; relapse 3.5 weeks; euthanised 9 weeks after diagnosis; no CSF drug level; authors: clinical-benefit literature for IT chemo in dogs limited to ~6 cases.
- Cats (not dog): JAVMA 2026;264(2) lomustine + corticosteroids in cats with presumptive CNS lymphoma (title only seen).

## (4) INTRATHECAL / INTRAVENTRICULAR
- HUMAN IT MTX CSF numbers: PMID 2809687 (Strother JCO 1989, 49 Ommaya courses, 12 pts, median dose 6 mg): median PEAK ventricular CSF MTX 423 uM; 24 h 4.6 uM; 48 h 1.05 uM; t1/2 5.7 h; >1 uM kept 24 h in all but 1 course, 48 h in half. Lumbar vs ICV (Shapiro NEJM 1975, search snippet, primary not opened): ventricular level after lumbar dose ~10% of ICV; Ommaya more reliable.
- DOG: PMID 582316 (Ramu 1979, Isr J Med Sci): IT MTX CSF + plasma PK model built from dog data (abstract only; no numbers). Dog IT MTX CSF concentration NOT FOUND.
- Dog safety: PMID 25041580 (in record): 112 dogs + 8 cats, 1 seizure. Dog cases: Front Vet Sci 2023 (NEW, above): cisternal MTX 2.5 mg + ara-C 100 mg, response 3.5 wk. Authors: IT benefit literature ~6 dogs, mostly ara-C alone.
- POTENCY: PMID 28992489 (Pawlak, Res Vet Sci 2017): MTX kills canine lines CLBL-1 (B), GL-1 (B), CL-1 (T); the T-cell line CL-1 was the MOST MTX-sensitive (stated in abstract). IC50 numbers: CLBL-1 <0.09 uM at 72 h and 'canine lymphoma lines IC50 2-3 nM' came from search snippet / PMC6827003 which cites a prior paper -> UNVERIFIED; full text not opened. Previously in record only as a title (SWEEP_escapes). => NEW use: IT MTX at CSF 1-400 uM exceeds any plausible IC50 by >=10x-1000x for >=48 h per dose (DERIVED; dog CSF volume not found, so dog CSF level is TRANSFER from human).
- IT thiotepa: PMID 2491958 (in record): IV thiotepa CSF:plasma AUC ~1 (n=1) -> thiotepa does NOT need IT delivery. IT thiotepa 10 mg: ventricular peak >100 ug/mL, ~10 at 2 h, ~1 at 8 h, t1/2 <30 min (Frontiers Pharmacol 2025;16:1472945 snippet, UNVERIFIED primary). Human recommended 2nd-line in leptomeningeal disease.
- IT rituximab / liposomal ara-C / DepoCyt / sustained release: ALREADY IN RECORD (SWEEP_regional s2, s5; H.3). Not repeated. IT rituximab is irrelevant to T-cell (CD20-negative).
- Ommaya/ventricular reservoir in dogs: still NOT FOUND (re-searched PubMed + web; web hits human only). Dog chronic intrathecal catheters exist for analgesia (PMIDs 18431131, 7856900, 1979915, 3592272 titles not read) -> catheter-tip granulomas already noted in lymphoma_toxicity_profiles.py:116.
- Cisternal vs lumbar in dogs: CSF collected cerebellomedullary 21 / lumbar 9 in oral-ocfosfate PK (PMID 37670479, in record).

## (5) INTRA-ARTERIAL
- Human: IA BCNU = 4x tumour drug level vs same IV dose; no survival benefit in phase III; vascular + neuro toxicity (visual loss, stroke, leukoencephalopathy) (search-snippet reviews; Springer 11864-005-0030-1, PMID 2997415 title). Dog selective IA brain chemo: NOT FOUND (BCNU mentioned in 1 dog glioma). IA MSC in 8 dogs safe (PMID 26415563, in record). Combined with osmotic BBBD, IA is the only form with durable human lymphoma data (Angelov 19451444).

## (6) INTRANASAL
- Dog: PMID 27553192 (Lowe, Drug Deliv Transl Res 2017; beagles N=6/dose, 24 and 192 IU/kg): intranasal insulin lispro detectable in CSF of beagles, below LOQ in humans (species difference; insulin, not chemo). Dog intranasal levetiracetam PK 2026 (JVPT; no CSF). Fraction of nasal dose reaching brain <1%; anti-Nogo-A antibody CSF/plasma ~1% at 24 h = not different from IV; pig FUS + intranasal anti-PD-L1 only 1.6x (PMC12712798, 2026, microhaemorrhages). Carmustine intranasal AUC brain 14.7x IV in rodent (snippet). => no lymphoma/chemo data in dog; WEAK; no active program.

## (7) P-gp
- DOG: ABCB1-1Delta: Collie 58%, Aus Shepherd 32%, Sheltie 31% allele freq; clinical hospital population 286 dogs: 1 heterozygote, del freq 0.2% (PMID? PMC9423603); genotyping-submitted population 599: 21.6% (PMC9423603). Dogs with ABCB1-1Delta: vincristine neutropenia p=.0005, thrombocytopenia p=.0001 (n=34 lymphoma dogs, PMID 18537875, Mealey 2008 JVIM; abstract via snippet, full abstract not opened) -> they get MORE systemic exposure/toxicity, brain exposure measured in dog = NOT FOUND for chemo drugs.
- Mouse KO brain fold: ivermectin 36-60x, selamectin 5-10x (PMID 19161460, Geyer 2009); 80-90x in older Cell 1994 (PMID 7910522, secondary snippet); vinblastine 3x sensitivity (snippet, UNVERIFIED as brain fold).
- Inhibitor in dog: PMID 28357033 (Ito, F1000Res 2017; IN RECORD as no benefit): oral valspodar 7.5 mg/kg q12h x5 d, doxorubicin first dose cut 30 -> 21 mg/m2; well tolerated; EFS 244 vs 240 d; OS 366 vs 369 d; no CNS data. Safe dose in dog = known.
- Inhibitor at human BBB: PMID? JNM 2009;50:1954 tariquidar 2 mg/kg in 5 volunteers: (R)-11C-verapamil DV +24+/-15%, K1 +49+/-36%: 'far from complete' inhibition. NHP valspodar 4.6x verapamil brain uptake (snippet). => P-gp inhibition at the BBB is a LOW-gain lever in primate (1.2-5x) and not applied in dogs for CNS.

## (8) PROPHYLACTIC / THERAPEUTIC CNS RADIATION (dog)
- IN RECORD: PMID 41420297 (10x4 Gy; 105 dogs; whole-brain gEUD <=30 Gy -> median OS 24.1 vs 12.2 mo; normal brain 30-40 Gy = strongest predictor of OS; toxicity not directly measured).
- NEW: PMC10069678 (Front Vet Sci 2023?): shortened WBRT 5x4 Gy (20 Gy) for MUO, 10 dogs, IMRT: no acute effects, no radiation damage in histology (only 1 dog >6 months, so long-term safety unproven), mean OS 723 d. Neutron-irradiation dog brain thresholds (Springer, epithermal neutron + BPA): whole-brain 6.7 Gy (9.8 Gy-Eq), hemisphere 8.2 Gy -> necrosis threshold (different radiation quality; do not transfer).
- Practice: half-body RT (HBI) PFI 1143 vs 316 d (PMC12082781, in record via 'half-body' mention; PMID not checked).

Focal, needle-electrode, ablative: not a diffuse-access method. (details beyond snippet not read)

## Human BBBD data extra: PMC9953868 (Finland, 25 first-line PCNSL, MATRix x1 then IA MTX 2.5 g/m2 + carboplatin 200 mg/m2 after 25% mannitol): CR 88%, 2-y OS/PFS 81% per-protocol (67.1%/70.3% in the other snippet = probably ITT; UNRESOLVED), median FU 30 mo, TRM 8%. ## READING FOR THE MODEL (my inference; not a finding from data)
- Per LYMPHOMA_UNIVERSE I.4/F, the T-cell brain stays open because E2 (dCK loss) and E5 (dormant, pump-armoured) are not reached by any division-gated agent EVEN at full access; raising access for doxorubicin/vincristine/cyclophosphamide/MTX lifts bulk kill, not E5. Access lifts matter for the NON-gated agents: verdinexor, HCQ, romidepsin (P-gp substrate: BBB opening does not remove pump), prednisolone (E1 defeats), antibodies (anti-CD20 irrelevant to T; a T-cell antibody does not exist in dogs), thiotepa (access already ~1). So test in the model: set CNS access to 0.3-1.0 for those agents and see if E5 closes; do not assume.
