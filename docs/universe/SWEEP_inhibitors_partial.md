CLAUDE.md rule 13: a class may be excluded only for a stated scientific reason (kill contradicted, defeated by a named escape, or toxicity unaffordable), never because canine data are absent; derive a TRANSFER or OUTCOME-calibrated input.

Method: kill rate k = ln(1 + C_free/IC50_free)/assay_days per day (the model's formula). C = time-averaged free exposure = (AUC_tau/tau)*fu. IC50_free = IC50_nominal * fu_medium, fu_medium = 1/(1+(1/fu-1)*0.10) (10% serum, assumed to bind like plasma; the convention already used in docs/universe/SWEEP_pk.md section 12). Human label PK from DailyMed SPL (fetched 2026-10-02, files in scratchpad/inh/*.txt). PMIDs verified by NCBI E-utilities efetch (same PubMed records as get_article_metadata); a subset re-checked with mcp__PubMed__get_article_metadata (35441128, 28619981, 28418267, 26059948). Full text read only where stated.

## C. BTK CLASS (ibrutinib, acalabrutinib, zanubrutinib, pirtobrutinib)

### C1. Canine data found (new relative to the repo record: the repo carried acalabrutinib only as ASSUMED 0.060 with "no cell-killing IC50")
- Ibrutinib IC50 in a canine DLBCL line (NEW; PMID 34884478, full text PMC8658042 read): "Based on the anti-proliferative effect of ibrutinib concentrations on CLBL-1 at 72 h, the IC50 was calculated as 1.3 uM." Also "No inhibitory effect was observed on the proliferation of any of the three cell lines for concentrations of ibrutinib lower than 0.1 uM"; "ibrutinib increased early apoptosis and late apoptosis/necrosis ... beginning at concentrations of 2.5 uM"; "5 uM ibrutinib significantly increased CLBL-1 apoptosis/necrosis from 19.3% up to 81.5%". CLBL-1 clusters with the ABC subtype (PMID 27434128). Ibrutinib at 1 uM alone reduced CLBL-1 proliferation by only 5% in the combination experiments.
- Acalabrutinib dog phase I (PMID 27434128, full text PMC4951150 read): 20 dogs, 2.5-20 mg/kg q12 or q24 h; "Overall response rate (ORR) was 25% (5/20) with a median progression free survival (PFS) of 22.5 days. Clinical benefit was observed in 30% (6/20)"; dogs achieving PR had PFS 56 d vs 22.5 d with SD. Canine PK (n=7, day 14): "The Cmax/Dose (mean+-STD) was 195+-94 ng/ml or 419+-201 nM, and the median (range) Tmax 6 (3,8) hours. Twelve hour exposures (AUC0-12hr) ranged from 3,086 to 31,445 hr*ng/ml with an average dose normalized AUC0-12hr of 1,005+-476 hr*ng/ml" (per mg/kg; the paper does not state the unit of dose-normalisation in the sentence; it is per mg/kg by construction). Target engagement: "Full BTK occupancy (>90% of available target) was achieved in peripheral B-cells at 3 hours after dosing on day 1 with a dosage of 2.5 mg/kg QD, for 5 of the 6 dogs"; pre-dose day 7 occupancy 83-99%; occupancy measured also in lymph-node FNAs. In vitro: BTK pathway inhibition "at drug concentrations as low as 0.01 uM following 1 hour"; proliferation reduced in CLBL1 "at concentration 1 uM". Safety: grade 1-2 anorexia, weight loss, vomiting, diarrhoea, lethargy; no DLT; prednisone co-administered in 16 dogs (10 of 16 had progressed on prednisone).
- Ibrutinib (PCI-32765) in dogs (PMID 20615965, Honigberg 2010 PNAS): abstract states "PCI-32765 induced objective clinical responses in dogs with spontaneous B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma." The n, ORR and duration are in the full text, which is NOT retrievable (PMC2919935: publisher does not allow XML; MCP full_text empty). NOT FOUND numerically.
- Ibrutinib in canine mast cells (PMID 31286638): IC50 0.1-1 uM primary MCT cells, 1-3 uM in C2 and NI-1 lines (different disease; shows the same uM scale for proliferation).
- Beagle plasma PK method paper for acalabrutinib/ibrutinib/zanubrutinib (PMIDs 32971496, 36094354): numbers not in the abstracts; NOT RETRIEVED.

### C2. Human lymphoma efficacy (verified)
- Ibrutinib MCL, pooled 3 trials, n=370, ~10 y follow-up (PMID 35441128, full text PMC9010121): "In patients who achieved a CR (N = 102), median PFS and DOR were 68.5 and 66.4 months, respectively, and median OS was NR with a 5-year OS rate of 83%"; "a notable number of patients had durable disease control for >5 years"; 2L ibrutinib (N=99): median PFS 25.4 mo, ORR 77.8%, CR 37.4%, median DOR 35.6 mo. Earlier phase 2 (PMID 26059948, n=111): ORR 67% (23% CR), median DOR 17.5 mo, 24-month PFS 31%.
- Ibrutinib relapsed CNS lymphoma, 46 pts, median follow-up 49.9 (PCNSL) and 62.1 (SCNSL) months (PMID 38995739): "Tumor responses were observed in 23/31 (74%) patients with PCNSL and 9/15 (60%) patients with SCNSL, including 12 complete responses in PCNSL and 7 in SCNSL. The median progression-free survival (PFS) for PCNSL was 4.5 months ... with 1-year PFS at 23.7% ... The median duration of response in the 23 PCNSL responders was 5.5 months." Phase I (PMID 28619981): "Clinical responses to ibrutinib occurred in 10 of 13 (77%) patients with PCNSL, including five complete responses. The only PCNSL with complete ibrutinib resistance harbored a mutation within the coiled-coil domain of CARD11"; CD79B-mutant tumours incomplete. French iLOC (PMID 31279304): n=52; DC 62% ITT, 10 CR (19%), 17 PR (33%); median PFS 4.8 mo; "Ibrutinib was detectable in the cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)". Pooled BTKi in CNS lymphoma, 40 studies, 935 patients (PMID 40931981): "Among patients with PCNSL, zanubrutinib achieved pooled ORR and CR rates of 85% and 54% ... Ibrutinib 67% and 46%".
- Zanubrutinib CSF (PMID 35004280, n=13): "The corrected mean CSF/plasma ratio determined based on 94% protein binding was 42.7% +- 27.7% (range, 8.6%-106.3%)"; "The mean peak concentration of zanubrutinib in CSF was 2941.1 pg/ml". This is CSF vs corrected (free) plasma, human, clinical.

### C3. Efflux / CNS
- Ibrutinib is a modest ABCB1 substrate (PMID 30247919, mouse): "ibrutinib was transported moderately by human ABCB1 and mouse Abcg2 but not detectably by human ABCG2"; "Abcb1 markedly restricted the brain penetration ... resulting in 4.5- and 5.9-fold increases in ibrutinib brain-to-plasma ratios in Abcb1a/1b-/- and Abcb1a/1b;Abcg2-/- mice". The Imbruvica label says in vitro "not a substrate of P-gp or BCRP". Conflict: label (in vitro screening) vs mouse/transwell data (moderate transport). Net: WEAK-MODERATE P-gp substrate, not BCRP (human). Ibrutinib also inhibits ABCB1/ABCC10 efflux function (PMID 28265007) so it does not behave like a classic pump-limited cytotoxic.


### B2. PI3K-delta: potency in lymphoma lines, kill-rate arithmetic, quiescent kill, CNS, toxicity, escapes

Potency inputs (HUMAN lines; no canine IC50 exists for any PI3K-delta drug, so this is a TRANSFER; the repo previously stopped at "inversion only"):
- Duvelisib, 20-line panel, 72 h ATP read-out (PMID 30067771, full text PMC6070190 read): "Fourteen of 20 cell lines tested showed at least 50% maximal growth inhibition in response to three days of duvelisib exposure with a median GI50 of 0.59 uM"; "Several cell lines display considerable sensitivity to duvelisib (GI50 values ranging from 0.5 to 200 nM), however, many are weakly sensitive"; "In six cell lines (RL, KARPAS-299, RPMI-8226, GRANTA-519, OCI-Ly7, OPM-2), the GI50 failed to reach levels of greater than fifty percent". Table 1: HBL-1 (ABC) 5.3 uM; OCI-Ly3 (ABC) 3.7 uM; U-2932 (ABC) 1.8 uM; Farage (GCB) 0.04 uM; TMD-8 (ABC) 0.0005 uM; cutaneous T-cell line HH 10 nM. "The greatest sensitivity was seen in the DLBCL or transformed FL cell lines, with less activity seen in MCL, MM or T-cell lymphoma lines."
- Duvelisib in 8 EBV+/- lines (PMID 29522278, PMC5911584 read): 0.1-5 uM, 72 h; "B cell lines were more susceptible to duvelisib than T and NK cell lines"; apoptosis and cell-cycle arrest "in EBV-positive and -negative B cell lines, but not in T cell lines".
- Idelalisib (GS-1101) cell-line IC50: NOT FOUND (ChEMBL returns only pAKT EC50 100 nM in CCRF-SB and SU-DHL-5, PMID 27561716). Idelalisib mantle cell lines: "A moderate loss of viability without any change in cell-cycle profile was observed" (PMID 27342398). Idelalisib on MCL cells: "inhibition by GS-1101 transiently inhibits AKT phosphorylation but not proliferation in MCL cells" (PMID 23676220).
- Re-phosphorylation of AKT by 24 h in DoHH2 under duvelisib alone, prevented by ibrutinib or dexamethasone (PMID 30067771): PI3K bypass (mTORC2) is built into the cell response.

Kill-rate arithmetic (script scratchpad/inh/calc.py; duvelisib 25 mg BID, label AUC 7.9 ug.h/mL per 12 h => 658 ng/mL => 1579 nM total (MW 416.87); fu < 0.02 => free 31.6 nM; fu_medium 0.169 (10% serum); 72 h):
- Weak/resistant line (HBL-1, 5.3 uM): C/IC50_free 0.035; k = ln(1.035)/3 = 0.0115 /day (exposure x0.5-x1.5: 0.0058-0.017).
- Median line (0.59 uM): C/IC50_free 0.316; k = 0.092 /day (x0.5-x1.5: 0.049-0.129).
- Sensitive line (Farage 0.04 uM): C/IC50_free 4.66; k = 0.58 /day (0.40-0.69).
- Because C_free/IC50_free is approximately 0.1 x C_total/IC50 when fu is small, the result does not depend on the unmeasured fu.
- Duvelisib kill is therefore a MIXTURE: ~0.01 /day in the unresponsive third to ~0.6 /day in the responsive third. Central (median-line) 0.09 /day is at the repo bar (~0.090/day). Grade TRANSFER (human lines + human label PK), spread 50x.
- Cross-check against the clinical outcome: human PTCL PRIMO ORR 48%, CR 33%, mPFS 3.4 mo; iNHL ORR 47%; canine RV1001 ORR 62-77% with TTP 21-25 days (PMID 29689086): the response FRACTION of ~half agrees with the "responsive subpopulation" reading; the short durability agrees with the PI3K-bypass/AKT re-phosphorylation reading.
- Idelalisib: human AUC 10598 ng.h/mL per 12 h => 883 ng/mL => 2126 nM total (MW 415.43), fu <= 0.16; no IC50, so no derived k. Grade ASSUMED for idelalisib unless the duvelisib panel is accepted as a class transfer (flag weaker: idelalisib is delta-only, duvelisib delta+gamma, and gamma matters in T-cell lines).

Quiescent / non-dividing cells:
- Evidence that PI3K-delta inhibition kills NON-cycling cells: "inhibition of PI3Kdelta induces apoptosis of primary MCL tumor cells once they have ceased to cycle ex vivo, and this killing is enhanced by PD 0332991 inhibition of CDK4/CDK6" (PMID 23676220, abstract; human primary MCL, ex vivo). Same paper: killing by GS-1101 is "cell cycle-dependent" in proliferating MCL cells (transient AKT inhibition, no proliferation effect) and magnified when cells sit in prolonged G1. So division-gating: NO for the arrested state; the effect is strongest in G1-arrested cells. Duvelisib: "decreased viability of primary human neoplastic B CLL cells ... These effects were modest" (PMID 30067771, citing refs 23, 24 = in-vitro CLL, no microenvironment). Grade: TRANSFER (human primary MCL, one paper, abstract-level).

CNS: no CSF:plasma or brain Kp,uu retrieved for idelalisib/duvelisib/copanlisib (PubMed 'idelalisib cerebrospinal fluid' returned 0). Both are P-gp/BCRP substrates (labels) with >84-98% plasma binding. Clinical CNS lymphoma: NOT FOUND for idelalisib/duvelisib (a PI3K-delta plus ibrutinib synergy exists in PCNSL cells, PMID 28619981, but no PI3K-delta single-agent CNS series retrieved). ME-401 (a different PI3K-delta compound) "present in brain tissue at 4 and 24 h" in mice (PMID 31506873; per SWEEP_pk.md). CNS access: NOT FOUND -> ASSUMED (low).

Toxicity axis and rates (labels and trials):
- Idelalisib boxed warnings (label): hepatotoxicity, severe diarrhoea/colitis, pneumonitis, infections, intestinal perforation (label section not re-extracted here; listed in docs/universe/SWEEP_pk.md s13). Withdrawn for FL/SLL in 2022 after "Serious adverse effects were reported in 2016 leading to termination of postmarketing registry trials" (PMID 36939665).
- Duvelisib: boxed warning infections, diarrhoea/colitis, cutaneous reactions, pneumonitis (Copiktra label; SWEEP_pk.md s13). Axes: immune/infection, GI (colitis), hepatic, skin, lung.
- Dog RV1001: hepatic (9/24 grade 3-4 ALT/AST at 15 mg/kg), GI grade 1-2 (PMID 29689086).

Escape coverage (what a PI3K-delta inhibitor is itself vulnerable to / covers):
- Vulnerable to: PI3K/AKT bypass (mTORC2 re-phosphorylation of AKT at 24 h, PMID 30067771); microenvironment IL-6 (PMID 31601188: "Interleukin-6 mediates resistance to PI3K-pathway-targeted therapy in lymphoma", title only read); PIK3CA/PI3K-gene variants (canine B-cell lines carry "potentially high-impact somatic variants in the genes that encode PI3K", PMID 34884478); P-gp/BCRP efflux (labels, in vitro); AKT hyperactivation via FoxO1/Rictor (PMID 39436708, CLL, title read).
- Covers: BCR/NF-kB dependent lines (synergy with ibrutinib, PMID 30067771, 34884478); TP53-independent (apoptosis in non-cycling cells reported, PMID 23676220; TP53 effect not retrieved).
- Does NOT cover: P-gp/BCRP (itself substrate), CD20/CD19 (independent of both), steroid resistance (no effect; duvelisib + dexamethasone synergy, PMID 30067771), dCK, topo2.

## D. IMiDs: lenalidomide, pomalidomide (and a species-specific CEREBLON finding)

NEW sequence finding (my own computation, UniProt, fetched 2026-10-02; needs wet-lab confirmation): canine cereblon (UniProt A0A8I3NIQ5, 444 aa) vs human CRBN (Q96SW2, 442 aa) vs mouse Crbn (Q8C7D2, 445 aa), pairwise-aligned with difflib: dog 97.3% similar (ratio 0.973), mouse 95.6%. In the C-terminal thalidomide-binding domain the only non-identical human residue in dog is human V388, and the dog local sequence is "...GYAWTIAQCKICAS" (Ile) whereas human is "...GYAWTVAQCKICAS" (Val); mouse has the same Ile at that position (plus E377V and others). The published mechanism: "mice with a single I391V amino acid change in Crbn exhibit thalidomide-induced degradation of drug targets ... Ikaros (Ikzf1), Aiolos (Ikzf3), Zfp91, and casein kinase 1a1" and "These molecules lack activity in murine models" (PMID 30064974, Blood 2018); "murine cereblon (CRBN), the substrate receptor for IMiD action, is resistant to some of IMiDs therapeutic effects" (PMID 30373817). Mouse "I391" corresponds to human "V388" (the human numbering printed in the CRBN literature differs by 3 between species; my alignment places the mouse and dog Ile at the same aligned position as human V388). INFERENCE: canine CRBN carries the mouse-type residue and is predicted to be IMiD-resistant. This is a sequence inference, not a measurement; no canine IMiD pharmacology retrieved (PubMed 'lenalidomide dogs', 'pomalidomide dog', 'cereblon canine' returned nothing relevant). This is a NAMED scientific reason under CLAUDE.md rule 13 (class defeated by a species-specific target difference), pending a canine IKZF1 degradation assay.

- Escapes: both are P-gp SUBSTRATES (labels; METTL7A/B thiol-HDACi resistance, PMID 38151817 title); romidepsin (a thiol prodrug) is vulnerable to METTL7A/B; both cover TP53-independent apoptosis (not retrieved).

## J. BCL2: venetoclax (add to repo record)

- Repo: canine T-cell EC50 23 +- 18 nM (n = 7), B-cell 288 +- 700 uM (so B-cell kill ~ 0); derived T-cell k 0.07-2.1/day (docs/universe/SWEEP_pk.md s12). Not redone.
- New human data: venetoclax monotherapy NHL phase 1 (n = 106; PMID 34083230): "median PFS for all 106 patients was 5.4 months (FL 10.8; MCL 11.3; MZL 21.2; WM 30.4). The median DoR was 14.9 months ... CR versus PR predicted longer DoR in both MCL (31.5 vs 10.1 months) and FL (37.6 vs 9.7 months)"; ORR 44%; neutropenia 19%. Ibrutinib + venetoclax in MCL, 7-year follow-up (AIM, n = 24; PMID 38662991): "estimated 7-year progression-free survival (PFS) was 30% (95% CI, 14-49; median, 28 months)"; "Eight patients in MRD-negative CR entered ETI for a median of 58 months (95% CI, 37-79), with 4 experiencing disease recurrence. Two of 3 reattained CR on retreatment". This is the only inhibitor-only regimen in this sweep with a human plateau-like tail (30% progression-free at 7 y; 8/24 patients in MRD-negative CR long enough to stop therapy), in a BCR-dependent, BCL2-dependent human disease; its dog analogue would need BOTH BTK dependence and B-cell BCL2 dependence, and the dog B-cell BCL2 measurement is the opposite (resistant).
- Resistance mechanisms: MCL1/BCL-XL rewiring (PMID 28140720 title only), BCL2 mutation; P-gp/BCRP substrate (label, repo).

## K. CDK4/6, mTOR, JAK/SYK, MDM2

### K1. CDK4/6: palbociclib (NEW canine IC50 found; the repo excluded CDK4/6 as "in vitro only ... no exposure")
- Canine IC50, 48 h CCK-8 (PMID 36450591, full text read; text mislabels the first palbociclib pair as abemaciclib; the pairing is palbociclib 17-71 1.6378 +- 0.1374 uM and GL-1 0.4060 +- 0.0957 uM): "In cell lines with low p16 (CLBL-1, CLC, Nody-1, and UL-1), the IC50 values of palbociclib were 0.0081 +- 0.0013, 0.0061 +- 0.0004, 0.0086 +- 0.0013, and 1.1742 +- 0.0844 (uM)"; abemaciclib 0.0053, 0.0068, 0.0089 and 0.1128 uM; high-p16 lines 17-71 and GL-1 resistant: "high p16 protein expression may be used as a biomarker for resistance to CDK4/6 inhibitor therapy". Cells treated 0.01-100 uM for 48 h.
- Human exposure (Ibrance label; palbociclib free base MW 447.5 [the ChEMBL 573.68 is the isethionate salt]): CL/F 63.1 L/h; so AUC24 at 125 mg = 125 mg / 63.1 L/h = 1981 ng.h/mL (derived from CL/F, assumes label CL/F is for this dose); on-treatment mean 82.5 ng/mL = 184 nM total; "Binding of palbociclib to human plasma proteins in vitro was approximately 85%" (fu 0.15) => 27.7 nM free; fu_medium 0.638; t1/2 29 h.
- Arithmetic (growth INHIBITION rate, not kill): low-p16 CLBL-1/CLC/Nody-1 (IC50 6.1-8.6 nM): C/IC50_free 5.0-7.1; k = ln(1 + r)/2 d = 0.90-1.05 /day (exposure x0.5-x1.5: 0.63-1.23); UL-1 (1.17 uM) 0.018 /day; high-p16 GL-1 0.051 /day, 17-71 0.013 /day.
- This is a CYTOSTATIC agent (G1 arrest): "Induction of prolonged early G1 arrest by CDK4/CDK6 inhibition" (PMID 23676220). Division-gated: YES by mechanism (acts only on cells that enter G1/S; quiescent G0 cells are not affected; no direct citation retrieved for a lymphoma quiescent-cell experiment). A growth-inhibition k does not remove cells; the model must treat it as a cycle-arrest with durable clearance only if cells die (e.g. with BTK/PI3K inhibitors: "prolonged G1 arrest ... robust apoptosis" with GS-1101, PMID 23676220; ibrutinib + palbociclib MCL phase 1 n = 27: "overall and complete response rates were 67% and 37%, and ... the 2-year progression-free survival was 59.4%", PMID 30692121).
- Efflux/CNS: "palbociclib is a substrate of both P-gp and BCRP"; brain-to-plasma ratios "0.3 in WT and Abcg2(-/-) mice versus 5.5 and 15 in Abcb1a/b(-/-) and Abcg2; Abcb1a/b(-/-) mice" (PMID 26123925). CNS access: 0.3 (mouse, total), TRANSFER.
- Toxicity: marrow (neutropenia 41% g3-4 and thrombocytopenia 30% in the MCL combination, PMID 30692121); rash DLT; dog: not retrieved.
- Vulnerable: high p16 / low pRb phosphorylation (canine 17-71, GL-1, UL-1), RB1 loss (not retrieved), P-gp/BCRP; covers: nothing in the TP53 list (palbociclib is TP53-independent but RB-dependent).

### K2. mTOR (sirolimus, everolimus)
- Human: everolimus in relapsed classical Hodgkin lymphoma n = 57: "Overall response rate was 45.6% ... five patients (8.8%) experienced a complete response" (PMID 29774169). Everolimus label: 74% bound, t1/2 ~30 h, P-gp/CYP3A4 substrate. Sirolimus solution label: P-gp and CYP3A4 substrate.
- Dog: oral rapamycin 0.1 mg/kg PK in 5 healthy dogs: AUC0-48 140 +- 23.9 ng.h/mL, Cmax 8.39 +- 1.73 ng/mL, t1/2 38.7 +- 12.7 h (single dose); 5 daily doses: AUC0-48 126 ng.h/mL, Cmax 5.49 ng/mL (PMID 26709938). Adjuvant sirolimus did not improve outcome in 324 dogs with osteosarcoma (PMID 33753454; a different disease). Canine lymphoma line IC50 for any mTOR inhibitor: NOT FOUND. Kill rate: not derivable (ASSUMED); mTOR inhibitors are cytostatic (G1) in lymphoma lines in general; no citation retrieved here for that statement.

Label: 97% bound, "Ruxolitinib was not a P-gp substrate". Canine JAK data = oclacitinib (repo): EO-1 cutaneous IC50 2.25 uM; CLBL-1 and 17-71 B-cell lines insensitive. So the class matches T-cell/large-granular/cutaneous disease only.

## M. Veterinary-licensed and veterinary-used agents

- Rabacfosadine (Tanovea; DailyMed 5b36e345-fae9-40ea-a069-6fefba92a89f): 1 mg/kg IV over 30 min q21d up to 5 doses. Randomised double-blind placebo-controlled n = 158 (PMID 34952995): "median PFS was significantly longer in the RAB group compared to placebo (82 vs 21 days)"; "The BORR for RAB-treated dogs was 73.2% (50.9% complete response [CR], 22.3% partial response [PR]) and 5.6% ... for placebo"; "37 RAB-treated dogs (33%) were progression free [one month after last treatment]". Phase I/II n = 38: "Antitumor responses were observed in 79% of dogs" (PMID 19417014). With L-asparaginase, relapsed n = 52: ORR 67%, MPFS 63 d, CR dogs 144 d (PMID 32064697). Mechanism: "GS-9219 is converted to its active metabolite, PMEG diphosphate, via enzymatic hydrolysis, deamination, and phosphorylation" and "resting lymphocytes and solid tumor lines were less sensitive" (PMID 18451250); "ADAL protein plays an essential role in the intracellular activation of GS-9219" and ADAL mutations (H286R, S180N) confer resistance (PMID 23645737). dCK INDEPENDENCE: the activation pathway named in these abstracts (hydrolysis, ADAL deamination, two phosphorylations of the phosphonate) contains no deoxycytidine-kinase step, because PMEG already carries the phosphonate that stands in for the first phosphorylation; the identity of the kinases that make PMEGpp was NOT RETRIEVED. So rabacfosadine is, by pathway, not defeated by dCK loss (escape E2 that removes cytarabine), but it has its own named escape: ADAL loss (canine prevalence NOT FOUND). Division-gated: YES (preferential cytotoxicity "in cells with a high proliferation index", PMID 19417014; resting lymphocytes less sensitive, PMID 18451250). Toxicity (label): "TANOVEA is associated with life-threatening or fatal pulmonary fibrosis"; contraindicated in West Highland White Terriers; neutropenia nadir ~7 d (grade 3: 8 instances, grade 4: 3 instances in the field study); GI (diarrhoea 87.5% in the RCT), dermatopathy (late cycles); SAE 20% (PMID 34952995). The repo carries it as ASSUMED 0.200 /day (docs/LYMPHOMA_COVERAGE_LEDGER.md); no IC50/PK pair retrieved (the abstract of PMID 18451250 gives no numbers; full text not open). Grade: OUTCOME-calibrated (randomised dog data), not potency-derived. P-gp: NOT FOUND.
- Toceranib (Palladia; dog label): "highly protein bound at 91% to 93%", t1/2 16.4 h, 3.25 mg/kg q48h Cmax 86-109 ng/mL, AUC0-48 1833-2635 ng.h/mL. Lymphoma: 5 dogs with MDR lymphoma, "two of the five subjects ... exhibited partial responses" (PMID 28592719). No lymphoma-line IC50 retrieved; targets (KIT, PDGFR, VEGFR) are not established lymphoma drivers (c-KIT higher in T-cell than B-cell lymphoma, PDGFRa higher in MDR T-cell, same paper). Kill rate: not derivable; grade ASSUMED; weak candidate.
- Masitinib (EU-licensed for dogs; SPC in repo): epitheliotropic lymphoma n = 10: "Complete remission occurred in 2 of 10 dogs and lasted for median 85 days. Five dogs went into partial remission for median 60.5 days" and "its effects are most likely not generated through the KIT receptor" (PMID 26364581). P-gp reversal threshold >= 1 uM vs fu 0.07 (repo). Not a kill-rate agent.
- L-asparaginase (enzyme; DailyMed Oncaspar ac7b6a6f-6ddb-48c6-8088-d8a8e3fea6ce human; canine use is off-label of human/E. coli products): kills by apoptosis after protein-synthesis arrest in a mouse lymphoma line and canine lymph node biopsy ("massive destruction of neoplastic cells by what was consistent with apoptosis" at 4 h; "it seems unlikely that protein depletion per se ... is the triggering event ... It is more likely that the suspension of cellular proliferation commits cells to apoptosis"; PMID 8485807). Canine evidence: 34 dogs COP vs 42 dogs COP + asparaginase: "did not significantly increase the likelihood of a clinical remission or prolong the initial progression-free interval" (PMID 15995158, retrospective). Resistance: anti-asparaginase antibodies in 3/10 dogs after one injection and 4/7 after repeats (PMID 23253146), reduced with PEG form. Not a P-gp substrate (protein; no citation needed beyond class). CNS: PEG-asparaginase lowers CSF asparagine: "Median CSF asparagine concentration decreased from a pre-treatment level of 5.3 umol/l to median levels <= 1.5 umol/l" (PMID 24702187, human children); canine CSF: NOT FOUND. Kill rate: not derivable from IC50/exposure (units IU); OUTCOME grade: no benefit as an add-on (PMID 15995158). This agrees with the repo's "EVALUATED, EXCLUDED".
- Verdinexor: see section F.
