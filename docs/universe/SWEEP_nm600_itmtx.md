- PMID 30820474 (Hernandez, Commun Biol 2019, doi 10.1038/s42003-019-0327-4, PMC6391402) [full text read]: 90Y-NM600 in mouse EL4 + human Hut-102 T-NHL. Uptake 4x higher in EL4 than normal T cells in vitro; tumour 9.35 %IA/g plateau at 24h (10.28 ex vivo 72h); liver 9.98; others <5 %IA/g. Tumour dose 2.69 Gy/MBq; liver 3.04. 9.25 MBq (=25 Gy tumour) CR 8/10 s.c.; 4.63 MBq=12.5 Gy; 2x4.63 MBq 10 d apart local control 3/5 CR. Disseminated EL4 (IV; progression = hind-limb paralysis, i.e. CNS/PNS involvement): 9.25 MBq median OS 20->28 d, CR 1/5 only; authors: Y-90 mean free path 3.9 mm "suboptimal to affect single cells or very small tumors"; durable response needed adaptive immunity (Rag2 KO: 20% durable). Base case (dog 2.5 mg, weekly): CSF peak ~176 uM, 24 h 1.9 uM, 48 h 0.44 uM; CSF MTX > 1 uM for ~1.4 d (35 h), > 100 nM ~3.0 d, > 25 nM ~3.9 d, > 3 nM ~5.4 d. (volume 0.5x: 1.9/3.5/4.4/5.8 d; 0.25x: 2.4/3.9/4.9/6.3 d.)

## CONCLUSIONS FOR THE MODEL
NM600 CNS: uptake/kill NOT FOUND as a measurement; the evidence is NEGATIVE-DIRECTION: (i) dog: 2 brain mets not visible on 86Y-NM600 PET a month before clinical diagnosis, authors say APCs "may not be suitable ... treating intracranial disease" (34383787); (ii) human CLR124 PET: no significant normal-brain uptake (28913154); tumours with disrupted barrier TBR ~9-10 (28913154); (iii) iPSC BBB model low permeability (27421304); (iv) IV 90Y-NM600 in mouse disseminated T-lymphoma with CNS/PNS signs: CR 1/5 (30820474). Use access ~0 for intact-barrier CNS (leptomeningeal/parenchymal quiescent), >0 only for Gd-enhancing lesion; kill from IV NM600 in CNS: NOT FOUND -> do not count. Body (non-CNS) use: dog tumour dose prescription >=2 Gy/administration with marrow ~1 Gy (tumour:marrow >=2); 14.6-30 MBq/kg (my arithmetic); 2 Gy = 0.16-0.63 e-folds in vitro (SF2 0.85-0.53, record) before in-vivo factor 1.9; so per-dose body kill small unless escalated (study says 'likely can be escalated'; marrow = DLT). Repeat dosing: yes (2 wk apart in one dog; UW PTCL study allows 2nd dose if response/SD at d21).
IT MTX: IC50 canine lines 2-3 nM (secondary citation of 28992489; exposure time unverified); older canine lines 28-122 nM (6109397). CSF: human 6 mg -> 423 uM peak, 1.05 uM at 48 h, t1/2 5.7 h; dog CSF terminal t1/2 5.2 h; dog dose 2.5 mg flat. Derived (division-gated, CSF cycling cells only): weekly dosing duty (C>=IC50) 0.4-0.8, capped kill 0.2-0.7/day; T-cell pulse penalty x3.4 -> 0.25-0.58/day weekly, 0.73-0.88 twice weekly.
Longest repeated IT: DOGS: 6 doses 2x/wk (n=1, anecdote); 112 dogs single-dose safety. HUMANS: Ommaya 50-280 instillations / reservoir, years (record: Peyrl 280 max; 9347977 median 46 punctures/pt); IT MTX leukoencephalopathy 29% at 2 y in adult CNSL with HD-MTX+WBRT (HR 4.5, dose-dependent) => the toxicity bar is cumulative dose and co-therapy.
