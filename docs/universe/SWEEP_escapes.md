# Independent escape audit: canine multicentric lymphoma durable-response model

Audit date 2026-09-30. Scope: the 14-escape list (P-gp, BCRP, TP53/apoptosis evasion, CD20 loss, CD19 loss,
BCR/NF-kB independence, PI3K/AKT bypass, persister, autophagy independence, T-cell exhaustion/microenvironment,
B-lineage switch, antigen-presentation loss, MGMT, CD7 loss) plus the separately modelled CNS sanctuary, CAR-T
persistence, treatment clock and toxicity budgets.

## 0. Bar, record check, method

**Success criteria, quoted (CLAUDE.md rule 2) and graded against exactly these:**
- Task: "enumerate EVERY mechanism by which a canine lymphoma can relapse or escape a curative-intent combination, so that a combination can be shown to close all of them" (the earlier pass was "not thorough").
- Standing standard: "make sure every mechanism and every escape is closed by either real data or rigorous model, potency, toxicity etc all need to be considered".
- "I'm okay with no specific data but if scientifically sound" (transfer allowed if the basis is written; graded TRANSFER, never MEASURED).
- "I'm not asking if it's been demonstrated, I know it's not." So "not shown in dogs" is **not** reported below as a finding. Evidence tiers are labels of input grade only.

**Tier labels used:** `DOG` = measured in dogs (canine lymphoma unless stated). `HUMAN` = human or mouse, to be transferred with a written basis. `THEORY` = mechanism plausible, no verified data in either species found here.
Verification: every PMID/DOI below was fetched from PubMed (PubMed MCP for some; NCBI E-utilities, same records, for compact pulls). "(A)" = abstract read. "(T)" = title/metadata only, so the claim is limited to what the title says. No PMID is cited from memory.

**Record check (rule 1), what I read:** `docs/LYMPHOMA_STATUS.md`, `LYMPHOMA_COVERAGE_LEDGER.md`, `LYMPHOMA_CLOSING_COMBINATION.md`, `core/lymphoma_catalogue.py`, `core/lymphoma_grounded.py` (partly), and `git grep` across all 5 remote branches (branch tip `3d568c0`). Findings:
- **Already covered (where):** CNS sanctuary (`LYMPHOMA_DURABLE_RESPONSE.md` s5); second cancers after cure (s7; PMID 21320018); CAR-T anti-mouse rejection (PMID 32002286, 35898541); "clonal evolution: no paired canine diagnosis-vs-relapse sequencing exists" (COVERAGE_LEDGER s1); chemo-selected stem-like cells PMID 30238600 filed as "a variant of the persister" (LEDGER s1); eye/testis/marrow-niche sanctuaries flagged **unscoped, no canine mechanistic data found** (STATUS "Known gaps"); founder lesions TP53 25.6% / TRAF3 58.1% / FBXW7 / POT1 (LEDGER s1).
- **Identified as a gap but no evidence gathered:** "glucocorticoid-receptor loss and cytarabine dCK loss are not separate escapes" (STATUS, "Correction (2026-09-30)"). This audit supplies the evidence (E1, E2 below).
- **Not covered anywhere (grep of all branches, lymphoma files):** NR3C1, TOP2A/topoisomerase, C528S, MCL1/BCL-xL, CAM-DR/stromal adhesion, CD47/SIRPa, lymphoid progenitor cells, asparaginase/ASNS, RFC/antifolate, MDR1-mutant dogs, per-pump substrate flags.

## 3. Corrections and caveats for each of the 14 existing escapes

1. **P-gp / ABCB1.** DOG-measured, but: (i) the catalogue note calls it "THE DOMINANT REAL RELAPSE MECHANISM and it sets the bar", whereas 25475167 (A) found ABCB1 up only "in a subset of dogs with B cell lymphoma" and pre-treatment ABC expression **not predictive**; 36010910 (A) found high P-gp activity in 47% of 79 B-cell dogs with **no** association with outcome. (ii) 28357033 (A) valspodar RCT null for EFS/OS and LPC counts (already recorded). (iii) 23820219 (A): SP phenotype not shifted by verapamil or FTC. (iv) MDR1-deletion dogs (14627096 (A), 20337920 (A)) show the toxicity cost of systemic P-gp loss (serum exposure +24%, gut +174% in a PBPK model), which corroborates the co-dose multiplier; I did not check the code's basis for 2.5. **Suggested regrade:** keep MEASURED for existence; flag "sufficiency unproven" for the bar.
2. **BCRP / ABCG2.** (i) The catalogue note cites "drug resistance occurred in 35/63 (55.6%)" as evidence for BCRP. In 25475167 (A) 55.6% is resistance of any kind; ABCG2 up-regulation was seen **only in the T-cell subset**, with ABCB1 up in a B-cell subset. (ii) **One `efflux_substrate` flag covers both pumps.** Per-drug pump specificity is not represented: methotrexate is a BCRP substrate (14500392), venetoclax is mixed (29441961: ABCB1/ABCG2 reduce intracellular levels only at high expression; 26927160, 32085398: venetoclax inhibits BCRP; 34475043: ABCB1-overexpressing cells did **not** resist venetoclax), doxorubicin is handled by both. The catalogue marks venetoclax `efflux_substrate=True` "conservatively"; this should become per-pump. **High-dose methotrexate and acalabrutinib are coded `efflux_substrate=False`; the methotrexate flag conflicts with 14500392; acalabrutinib substrate status was not verified here.** (iii) The "pump" agent's evidence is P-gp (PSC833/valspodar, TGF-b inhibitor lowering P-gp, 33961622 (A)); it is not shown to address ABCG2, the T-cell pump.
3. **TP53 / intrinsic-apoptosis evasion.** The catalogue grades it `TRANSFER (general oncology)` and says "Not genotyped routinely". It is **MEASURED in dogs**: 26678182 (A): 7/43 (16%) p53-mutant (exons 4-8, SSCP), response rate **33% vs 88%**, OS **67 vs 264 d**; 39282821 (A): p53 IHC positive 27% (n=37), IHC predicts mutation with 97.3% accuracy, but in multivariable analysis only treatment, not p53 IHC, was significant; 39922874 (A): 25.6% by WGS; germline p53 mutations documented in dogs (9464543 (T)). Human: TP53 and KMT2D mutated in the majority of relapsed/refractory DLBCL and **clonally persistent** from diagnosis (32589730 (A)). **Structural caveat:** the note lumps BCL2 inhibition and glucocorticoids under TP53 loss. GR loss (E1), BCL2-family rewiring (E4) and TP53 loss are different lesions with different defeat sets; split them. I did not verify here that GC-induced lymphocyte apoptosis is p53-independent (from general knowledge, unverified), so treat that as a hypothesis to check.
4. **CD20 antigen loss.** DOG (per record PMID 32002286: exons 4-5 absent). HUMAN adds three distinct routes not modelled: MS4A1 transmembrane missense mutations that are rare at diagnosis but expand after rituximab from **minor pre-existing subclones** and are detectable in ctDNA (32589730 (A)); 5'-UTR isoform V1 limiting translation (37683180 (A)); promoter regulation with a surface-density **threshold** for complement killing (22228637 (A)); PDK4-mediated MS4A1 down-regulation (34252986 (A)). The model treats loss as binary; see E7. No trogocytosis/internalisation evidence was found (section 4).
5. **CD19 antigen loss.** In the largest human dataset found (42671910 (A), n=301) relapse-time CD19 loss is **mostly down-modulation, not deletion** (locus loss 8% -> 11%, n.s.; no coding mutations). So this is best modelled as E7 (antigen-low) plus a small null fraction. No canine relapse data found.
6. **BCR / NF-kB signal independence.** DOG-measured genetics: TRAF3 mutated in 44% (30% somatic) of 84 tumours (25468570 (A)), 36% (n=24; 33323169 (A)), 58.1% (WGS, n=43; 39922874 (A)). **Caveat:** 40506464 (A; 238 dogs) reports TRAF3 mutation **associated with prolonged PFS and OS in B-cell lymphoma**, so TRAF3 loss is a prognostic-favourable founder lesion for CHOP; its role as an "escape" is specific to BTK inhibition (acalabrutinib, ORR 25%, PFS 22.5 d). It also links to GC resistance: NF-kB activation down-regulates GR in canine lines (20362310 (A)). BTK C481S/PLCG2 (30760464 (A), 33154951 (A)) is functionally captured because NF-kB sits below BTK.
7. **PI3K/AKT bypass.** Now partly measured: 40506464 (A): **PIK3CD mutations inferior in T-cell lymphoma**; BTK + PI3K inhibitors synergise in canine DLBCL cells (34884478 (T), 40351764 (T)). No canine PTEN result found (E-section 4). AKT inhibition reverses GC resistance in ALL (24291004 (T)), a link to E1.
8. **Persister (non-dividing).** The record files 30238600 under this escape. I dissent in part: 21777289 (A) shows a **hierarchy** (LPCs share the bulk's clonal rearrangement and regenerate tumour on serial xenografts), and 23167606/23820219 (A) show the SP/LPC compartment is **efflux-high**. A persister is a reversible non-dividing state; an LPC is a self-renewing source that is also pump-high. Under the model's flags this "conjunction clone" (E5) is killed by fewer agents than either parent, so collapsing it into the persister **overstates closure**. Human lymphoma persisters exist and have a metabolic dependency: pirtobrutinib-tolerant MCL "giant cell" persisters are non-proliferative and TCA-cycle-addicted, reversible on drug removal (40700581 (A), abstract truncated).
9. **Autophagy independence.** ASSUMED in the record; this audit found **no** canine or human lymphoma evidence that autophagy-independent survival drives relapse. It remains a symmetry placeholder. Closure by HCQ hinges on an unmeasured canine IC50 (record).
10. **T-cell exhaustion / immunosuppressive microenvironment.** DOG evidence exists for the components: PD-L1 high in 81% of B-cell lymphomas, MFI ratio 1.50 (39132435 (A)); higher PD-L1/PD-1 scores predict relapse and death in chemotherapy-only dogs (n=33; 34209830 (A)); chemo-resistant lines raise PD-1/PD-L1 (29380929 (A)); tumour EVs kill CD8 cells and induce regulatory phenotype (37884996 (A)); tumour T cells few and hypo-responsive (22293625 (A)). Treg data conflict (22293625 up vs 28735665 down). It is a bundle of sub-mechanisms (E18). The escape is also not the only determinant of human CAR-T failure: PAX5/IRF8 (identity), **CD274**, **TMEM30A** were associated with CAR19 resistance (36584673 (A)), and CD58 aberrations trend higher in CAR-T progressors (37391168 (A)).
11. **B-lineage identity switch.** The catalogue grades this ASSUMED at 1e-9. There is HUMAN evidence that it is a real, **immunotherapy-selected** route: 2 of 9 post-CAR19 relapses (22%) switched to T-cell or histiocytic phenotype with identical clonal Ig rearrangement and B-cell promoter methylation (34799485 (A)); 75 lineage-switch relapses after CD19/CD22/CD38/CD7 therapy, 71% B-ALL to AML (40193715 (A)); follicular lymphoma to histiocytic/dendritic sarcoma with shared IGH/BCL2 rearrangement and PAX5 loss in 8 patients (18272816 (A)); PAX5/IRF8 alterations tied to CAR19 resistance (36584673). **Suggested regrade:** ASSUMED -> HUMAN-transfer, with the rate conditional on using lineage-directed effectors. Dogs: no relapse switch found; CD3+/CD20+ large-cell lymphomas exist at diagnosis (10/10 monoclonal TCR-gamma, 3/10 also IgH; 37837199 (A)); transformation of indolent disease is recognised but rare (26664970 (A)).
12. **Antigen-presentation loss.** DOG-measured prognostic: low MHC-II predicts relapse and death in 160 dogs (21781170 (A)). HUMAN: CD58 down in a large share of lymphomas across subtypes (n=748, 37391168 (A)); EZH2 inhibition restores silenced CD58 (31962268 (T)); CIITA/EP300 mutations in testicular LBCL (39380845 (A)). Serum B2M and IL-2R were significantly higher in relapsed dogs (17 lymphoma dogs; 36635848 (A), DOI 10.1111/vco.12873); that is a serum marker and must not be read as cell-surface loss.
13. **MGMT.** DOG-measured in vitro (26130852 (A)): 2/5 lines lomustine-resistant, MGMT inhibitor sensitised, expression **not** explained by promoter methylation. Missing mirror: **mismatch-repair deficiency** (tolerance of alkylation damage) and therapy-induced mutagenesis; canine MMR-protein loss is reported for tumours generally with lymphoma lower than melanoma (34538134 (A)); no canine lymphoma MMR-resistance data found. Low priority unless lomustine/temozolomide is a closing agent.
14. **CD7 antigen loss.** Per record 4/6 post-CD7-CAR relapses lost CD7 (37020231). Canine T-lineage antigens are aberrantly expressed in some cases (17981339 (T/partial A)). A T-lineage effector alternative not in the catalogue: **TRBC1/TRBC2 clonal-constant-region targeting**, which spares the TRBC2 half of normal T cells; canine TRBC1/2 sequence diversity and expression in lymphoid lines is characterised (41236719 (T)), and a human TRBC1-CAR phase 1/2 in PTCL exists (39528665 (T)). Relevant to the record's "missing object" for T-cell CNS disease; title-level evidence only. A TRBC-directed effector has its own loss route (TCR/TRBC down-regulation), not evaluated here.

## 5. One-paragraph verdict, at the strength shown

The 14-escape list is incomplete in ways that matter for closure, not just in count. Two gaps were already named by the user and the status file and now have evidence: **glucocorticoid resistance (E1)** is measured in dogs (NR3C1 alpha down in 6/6 high-grade dogs, resistance "essentially universal", median 68 d) and **nucleoside-activation loss (E2)** is measured in human lymphoma (dCK down in 50% of primary araC failures, cross-resistant to other nucleoside analogues) and is load-bearing for the cytarabine arms. Newly raised: **TOP2A loss (E3)** and **BCL2-family rewiring (E4)** each remove the one agent that anchors the smallest programs without touching a pump; the **lymphoid progenitor / efflux-high conjunction clone (E5)** is measured in dogs as a hierarchy that is also pump-high and valspodar-refractory, so filing it under "persister" overstates closure; the single `efflux_substrate` flag hides that **T-cell drug resistance is ABCG2-linked while the "pump" agent's evidence is ABCB1-only** and that P-gp is not shown sufficient for the bar (47% high activity at diagnosis, not predictive). Several items the prompt listed are not independent escapes or lack any verifiable data (BTK C481S, complement regulators, NK shedding, FAS loss, trogocytosis, masking, microbiome). All new entries are graded DOG, HUMAN-transfer or THEORY in Table A; none is shown to close or fail to close anything until it is given a potency and a seeding rate in the model. Modelled, not demonstrated, for every item.
