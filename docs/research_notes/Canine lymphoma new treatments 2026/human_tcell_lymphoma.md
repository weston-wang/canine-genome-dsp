## Q1. New FDA/EMA/NMPA/PMDA approvals for PTCL, CTCL, T-ALL in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
No new molecular entity was approved by FDA for any T-lineage lymphoma/leukaemia in the window, so the window adds no new extra-label agent for a US vet. The only FDA T-cell action was a ready-to-use generic romidepsin solution for CTCL (4 Jun 2026). Golidocitinib (China 2024) and valemetostat (Japan) stay non-US; duvelisib-PTCL, soquelitinib, lacutamab and WU-CART-007 are all still pre-approval; no CD7 CAR-T has NMPA marketing approval.

## Q2. CD7 / CD5 / CD4 / CD30 / CCR4 / TRBC1-2 / CD70 / CD28 CAR-T and engagers, 2025-2026; fratricide-resistant and off-the-shelf products; durability beyond 2 years

### Takeaway
CD7 CAR-T reached its first 5-year survival report (donor-derived, T-ALL, Haematologica 8 Oct 2026, POST-SWEEP, abstract not released), while three independent 2026 papers now document how it fails: CD7-antigen loss, lineage switch, and a new late EBV+ lymphoproliferative disorder. New or newly matured targets in the window: CD5-knockout autologous CD5 CAR (phase 1, ORR 85.7%), CD28 CAR (preclinical, spares CD8/NK), TRBC2 ADC (preclinical), CD70 allogeneic CAR CTX130 (ATLL ORR 50%, median PFS 2.5 mo), third-generation CD30 CAR (3-y PFS 74.2%, mostly Hodgkin) and AFM13-preloaded cord-blood NK (ORR 92.9%, 2-y EFS 26.2%). No CD4 CAR clinical data and no TRBC1 engager data appeared in the window. Beyond-2-year durability in T-lineage disease still depends on allo-HSCT consolidation after the CAR.

### Inferences
- The strongest new mechanism-level item for the dog program is CD28: it is a non-CD7, non-CD5 T-lineage target that spares CD8 and NK cells, and it is described as uniformly up-regulated in nodal TFH lymphomas. Canine PTCL-NOS is largely CD4+, so a CD4-sparing/CD4-depleting profile is close to the lineage the project cares about - but the data are mouse/in-vitro only, and no anti-canine-CD28 CAR exists.
- Durability data continue to show the same shape as the record's: high CR rates with CD7/CD5 CAR, then relapse unless consolidated with allo-HSCT. The project's existing DLA-identical allogeneic transplant arm is therefore the right scaffold; the new 2026 papers add a specific warning (residual CAR transgene at transplant predicts mixed chimerism with cytopenia) that applies to any CAR-plus-transplant canine design.
- Two of the newly surfaced modalities avoid cell manufacturing entirely and are therefore easier to imagine in dogs: the anti-TRBC2 ADC (an antibody plus payload) and the AFM13-style engager-armed NK approach. Both are at preclinical or human phase 1 only.

### Gaps
- The two most relevant durability readouts could not be quantified: the 5-year CD7 CAR-T paper (PMID 42845047) and the haplo-CD7 CAR paper (PMID 42501316) have no PubMed abstract, and the journal page for the former returned 404.
- ASH 2026 abstracts (which would carry the next CD7/CD5 CAR and TRBC1 updates) were not available at the time of this sweep.
- CTX130's PTCL/CTCL-cohort durability (median DoR, PFS) was not found; only the ATLL long-term abstract and 2022 topline percentages.

## Q3. Agents that kill non-dividing / quiescent T-lymphoma cells

### Takeaway
The window produced no new clinical agent with direct evidence of killing quiescent or leukaemia-initiating T-lineage cells. Every division-independent kill with clinical data in the window is immune or payload based: CAR-T/NK, ADC payload, immunotoxin, graft-versus-lymphoma. The only item naming the initiating-cell compartment is a preclinical anti-pre-TCR antibody-drug conjugate for T-ALL.

### Cited findings
- Pre-TCR identified as a marker of T-ALL leukaemia-initiating cells; pre-TCR signalling was required for initiating-cell activity in patient xenografts, and an anti-pTalpha antibody-drug conjugate inhibited that activity and tumour progression in mice. Preclinical; restricted to pre-TCR-positive T-ALL, so it does not apply to mature PTCL. — [PMID 40890517, doi 10.1038/s41590-025-02265-w](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40890517/). NOT IN RECORD (grep "pre-TCR" = 0).
- POST-SWEEP. Cochrane review of allogeneic transplant in adult mature T-cell lymphoma, search to Oct 2025: one first-line randomised trial (n=103) and two salvage trials (n=119). First-line allo versus auto showed little or no difference in 2-year overall survival (HR 1.3, 95% CI 0.7-2.4) or PFS (HR 0.9, 0.5-1.5), with a large increase in treatment-related mortality (RR 18.70, 1.11-315.72); 84% had acute and 47% chronic GVHD. In relapsed non-cutaneous disease (n=17) 2-year OS was about 80% and PFS about 64%, response 76%, 2-year treatment-related mortality about 6%. In relapsed CTCL (n=102) allo-SCT may increase PFS (HR 0.38, 0.21-0.69) with little effect on OS (HR 0.54, 0.23-1.29). All outcomes rated low to very low certainty. — [PMID 42905121, doi 10.1002/14651858.CD014672.pub2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42905121/). NOT IN RECORD. Directly relevant: the project's T-cell program leads with DLA-identical allogeneic transplant, and this is the strongest-design human evidence base for that choice, graded weak.
- Allogeneic transplant in PTCL narrative review: 5-year overall survival approximately 40-55% in selected patients, with non-relapse mortality and GVHD the limiting factors; reduced-intensity conditioning widened eligibility without losing disease control. — [PMID 42373849, doi 10.1038/s41409-026-02962-6](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42373849/). NOT IN RECORD.
- Epigenetic regimens versus conventional chemotherapy in relapsed/refractory PTCL, meta-analysis of 38 studies and 2,170 patients: pooled response 46% versus 47% (p=0.77, no difference), but 1-year PFS 28% versus 20% (p<0.01) and 1-year OS 57% versus 41% (p<0.01); the PFS advantage held for epigenetic monotherapy (26% versus 20%, p=0.04) and was largest at one prior line (35% versus 20%). — [PMID 42875907, doi 10.4143/crt.2026.0505](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42875907/); also TCLF 2026 abstract 30. NOT IN RECORD. Relevance: this is the best quantitative statement in the window on how far HDAC/DNMT/EZH-class agents, including the project's romidepsin, carry durability; it is an improvement in PFS without an improvement in response rate.
- All-oral parsaclisib (PI3K-delta) plus chidamide (HDAC) with step-down maintenance, phase 1b, n=12 relapsed/refractory PTCL: response 58% (95% CI 28-85), complete response 42% (15-72); at 26.8 months median follow-up, 2-year PFS 33.3% and 2-year OS 66.7%; one grade 3 hepatotoxicity dose-limiting toxicity, no grade 4 treatment-related events; TET2 alteration and NK-cell expansion correlated with durable remission. — [PMID 42827632, doi 10.1002/mco2.70993, NCT05083208](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42827632/). NOT IN RECORD. This is the longest follow-up in the window for an all-oral chemotherapy-free PTCL doublet, and the step-down maintenance design matches the project's continuous-oral-control model.
- Linperlisib plus chidamide, multicentre phase 1 in relapsed/refractory PTCL (Leuk Lymphoma, Apr 2026). ALREADY IN RECORD (SWEEP_tcell_agents.md:17, PMID 41773975). Supporting preclinical synergy in NK/T-cell lymphoma via PI3K/AKT/mTOR — [PMID 42758871](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42758871/), NOT IN RECORD.
- Tolinapant (ASTX660, IAP antagonist): the in-window clinical publication is a phase 1 in advanced solid tumours with pembrolizumab, not T-cell lymphoma. — [PMID 42957672, Nat Commun 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42957672/). The IAP/SMAC-mimetic class is ALREADY IN RECORD as assessed-and-unquantified (SWEEP_nongated.md:52: "no clinical-stage agent with lymphoma-line IC50 + exposure retrieved"); this phase 1 is NOT IN RECORD and does not supply a T-cell-lymphoma number either. IAP antagonism is a non-division-gated apoptosis mechanism, but no new T-cell-lymphoma efficacy data appeared in the window.
- NAMPT inhibition alone and with belinostat in T-cell lymphoma lines (TCLF 2026 abstract 20; preclinical cell-line screen). — [TCLF 2026 archive](https://2026tclf.dryfta.com/abstract-archive). NOT IN RECORD. NAD depletion is division-independent in principle; no exposure or canine data.

### Inferences
- Under the project's framing, the window adds no agent that closes the dormant/quiescent T-progenitor gap. The one target that is explicitly an initiating-cell target (pre-TCR) is thymic-stage and so is irrelevant to canine PTCL-NOS and T-zone lymphoma, though it would be on-lineage for canine lymphoblastic T-cell lymphoma.
- The epigenetic meta-analysis supports keeping romidepsin as a PFS-extending rather than response-improving component, which matches how the record already uses it.
- The parsaclisib-chidamide 2-year PFS of 33.3% is the clearest in-window benchmark for what continuous oral doublets achieve in humans; it is below the project's multi-year target, consistent with the record's conclusion that oral agents bridge rather than cure.

### Gaps
- No agent in the window reports a measured effect on non-cycling T-lymphoma cells. The record's existing statement that no candidate has direct evidence on the dormant T-lineage progenitor (SWEEP_tcell_agents.md:41) is unchanged by this sweep.

## Q5. For each item: FDA-approved now (usable extra-label)? Canine data? Target conserved in dog?

### Takeaway
Of everything new in the window, only the romidepsin ready-to-use solution is FDA-approved, and romidepsin is already in the program. Denileukin diftitox-cxdl is the one approved-and-therefore-lawful agent that the record has never evaluated, and it is the only new agent with actual in-vivo canine data, from the Seattle DLA transplant model where it depleted canine regulatory T cells but failed to prevent GVHD. Canine target conservation is documented for CD5, CD30, CD25 and TRBC1/2; it is explicitly missing for CD7; and canine TRBC extracellular sequences are identical between TRBC1 and TRBC2, which blocks the TRBC-selective strategy in dogs.

### Cited findings
- Denileukin diftitox (Ontak, DAB389-IL-2), canine in-vivo data. In the DLA-non-identical canine transplant model, 9 micrograms/kg IV on days 2-10 with methotrexate gave a median survival of 16 days versus 20 days for 35 historical methotrexate controls, and 5 of 6 dogs had three-system GVHD: it "did not prevent, mitigate, or delay acute GVHD". — [PMID 16920555, doi 10.1016/j.bbmt.2006.05.005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16920555/). In DLA-identical mixed-chimerism dogs, a single 18 micrograms/kg IV dose depleted regulatory T cells, and in vitro 10 nM denileukin diftitox abolished canine Treg suppression of T-cell proliferation (proliferation up by a median 300%, range 200-425%); donor chimerism did not change. — [PMID 22173275, doi 10.1016/j.vetimm.2011.11.009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22173275/). Both NOT IN RECORD (grep for both PMIDs and "Ontak" = 0). Significance: canine high-affinity IL-2 receptor binds the human fusion toxin, a tolerable canine IV dose is on record (9-18 micrograms/kg), and the current product Lymphir is FDA-approved, so this is lawful extra-label today. It has never been tested against canine T-cell lymphoma, and canine CD25 expression in T-cell lymphoma subsets would decide the target fraction.
- Canine CD25 in T-cell lymphoma: canine PTCL gene-expression and cell-of-origin work and nodal T-zone immunophenotyping exist — [PMID 38166662](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38166662/), [PMID 33208032](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33208032/). Both NOT IN RECORD. Neither was read for a CD25-positive fraction in this sweep.
- Canine TRBC1/TRBC2: a second independent canine study (14 breeds, 6 canine haematopoietic lines) found "no sequence diversity in the extracellular domain of TRBC1 and TRBC2", near-equal TRBC1 and TRBC2 mRNA in canine PBMC across breeds, skewed expression in lines, and germline TRBC transcripts in some canine B-cell lines (CLB70, GL1) but not CLBL1; the authors conclude the fully conserved canine extracellular sequence "presents a challenge for the development of differential therapeutic antibodies". — [PMID 41236719, doi 10.1111/vco.70003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41236719/). This PMID appears in the record only in a titles-only list (SWEEP_escapes.md:141), so its content is NOT IN RECORD. It directly contradicts the feasibility in dogs of both the human TRBC1 CAR (record: PMID 39528665) and the new anti-TRBC2 ADC (PMID 41429932).
- Canine CD7: no anti-canine CD7 clone found; ALREADY IN RECORD (SWEEP_dog_programs.md:53). Nothing in the window changes this, so every CD7-directed advance above, including the 5-year survival report and the CD7 CAR-NK platform, still has no canine reagent.
- Canine CD5: validated anti-canine CD5 monoclonal antibodies exist (DH3B, DH13A), with the caveat that CD5 loss is the most common aberrancy in canine T-NOS lymphoma. ALREADY IN RECORD (SWEEP_dog_programs.md:52). The new human CD5-knockout CAR (CT125A, phase 1) and the low-affinity CD5 CAR therefore have a canine binder available but a known antigen-loss problem.
- Canine CD30: POST-SWEEP. Eight canine digital CD30-positive MUM1-positive non-epitheliotropic T/NK-cell lymphomas were characterised by immunohistochemistry; all expressed at least one T/NK marker, CD3 was positive in only 3 of 8, and TRG clonality was found in 5 of 8. — [PMID 42935134, doi 10.1177/03009858261500108](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42935134/). NOT IN RECORD. Significance: canine CD30 immunohistochemistry is established (earlier canine anaplastic large-cell lymphoma reports: [PMID 29455626](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29455626/), [PMID 31170900](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31170900/), both NOT IN RECORD), so a CD30 target fraction exists in a defined canine subset; the record's position that no dog CD30 antibody therapeutic exists (SWEEP_tcell_agents.md:5) is unchanged.
- Canine CD28, CD70, KIR3DL2: no canine therapeutic reagent or expression study was found for any of them in this sweep. All NOT IN RECORD.
- Canine JAK1 axis: POST-SWEEP-adjacent. A dog with Sézary syndrome responded to oclacitinib dose-dependently (0.7 mg/kg BID, improvement by day 15 with disappearance of Sézary cells, relapse day 32, 3 mg/kg BID then improvement within 12 days, relapse day 63), with JAK1 expression confirmed by immunohistochemistry. — [PMID 41473101, doi 10.3389/fvets.2025.1645059](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41473101/). ALREADY IN RECORD (SWEEP_modalities.md:97 and the citation list at SWEEP_modalities.md:228). This is the canine-side counterpart of the human golidocitinib MF case reports above, and supports JAK1 as a conserved, druggable canine T-lymphoma dependency with short duration.
- Canine EZH2: overexpression in canine T-cell lymphoma lines enhanced colony formation and suppressed apoptosis. — [PMID 42368342, doi 10.3389/fvets.2026.1823343](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42368342/). ALREADY IN RECORD (SWEEP_inhibitors_partial.md:156, SWEEP_modalities.md:102), which also records that no canine EZH2-inhibitor experiment exists. Valemetostat remains non-US-approved, so it is not lawfully usable anyway.
- Canine T-cell lymphoma lines named in the project (OSW, CL-1, UL-1, EMBLY): no in-vitro test of any agent new in this window was found on any of these lines. The canine lines appearing in the window's papers are different ones (CLB70, GL1, CLBL1 in the TRBC study; CLC, Ema, Nody-1 in the cabazitaxel study already in record; EO-1 in the oclacitinib work already in record).
- Canine tyrosine-kinase-inhibitor meta-analysis (11 trials, 101 dogs; response in T-cell 25/39 = 0.63 versus 0.27 in B-cell) ALREADY IN RECORD (SWEEP_tcell_agents.md:14, PMID 42656497).

### Inferences
- Ranked by "lawful today plus some canine evidence plus conserved target", the window's only genuinely new candidate for the dog program is denileukin diftitox-cxdl: FDA-approved, a canine tolerable IV dose published, canine high-affinity IL-2 receptor binding demonstrated in vitro and in vivo, and a human T-cell lymphoma indication. Its human response rates are low (16.3% in the Japanese post-marketing cohort) and capillary leak syndrome is the dose-limiting toxicity, so it is a Treg-depletion or adjunct agent rather than a bulk-kill agent.
- Every other advance in the window is either not approved (so unlawful for a vet), or lacks a canine binder (CD7, CD28, CD70, KIR3DL2), or is blocked by canine biology (TRBC1/TRBC2 extracellular identity).
- The CD5 route is the T-lineage cell-therapy path with the best canine reagent position, and the window strengthened its human side with a first-in-human CD5-knockout CAR and a non-editing low-affinity alternative. It remains limited by canine CD5 loss in PTCL-NOS.

### Gaps
- No canine pharmacokinetics were found for any agent new in the window, and no in-vitro data on OSW, CL-1, UL-1 or EMBLY.
- The canine CD25-positive fraction in PTCL-NOS, T-zone and lymphoblastic T-cell lymphoma was not quantified here, so the addressable fraction for denileukin diftitox in dogs is unknown from this sweep.
- Canine CD28 and CD70 expression in T-cell lymphoma: no study found either way.
