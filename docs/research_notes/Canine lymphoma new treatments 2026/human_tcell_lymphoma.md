## Q1. New FDA/EMA/NMPA/PMDA approvals for PTCL, CTCL, T-ALL in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
No new molecular entity was approved by FDA for any T-lineage lymphoma/leukaemia in the window, so the window adds no new extra-label agent for a US vet. The only FDA T-cell action was a ready-to-use generic romidepsin solution for CTCL (4 Jun 2026). Golidocitinib (China 2024) and valemetostat (Japan) stay non-US; duvelisib-PTCL, soquelitinib, lacutamab and WU-CART-007 are all still pre-approval; no CD7 CAR-T has NMPA marketing approval.

TFH-type retrospective series n=7: ORR 86% (1 CR, 5 PR) — [PMID 42192890, doi 10.3390/cancers18101529](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42192890/). Medical Letter reviews Feb/May 2026 — [PMID 41649365](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41649365/), [PMID 42068317](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42068317/). Record holds the word "denileukin" only, as a grep-zero note (SWEEP_tcell_agents.md:6); all data and the approval are NOT IN RECORD.

### Inferences
- The lawful extra-label T-cell menu for a US vet on 31 Oct 2026 is unchanged in substance from the project's October sweep; only a more convenient romidepsin vial is new. The one approved agent the record has not evaluated is denileukin diftitox-cxdl (FDA-approved Aug 2024), so it is lawful today.
- The next plausible lawful additions all fall outside this window: soquelitinib (phase 3 interim pending), lacutamab (accelerated BLA after TELLOMAK 3 starts), WU-CART-007 (BLA 2027), golidocitinib (no US filing date).
