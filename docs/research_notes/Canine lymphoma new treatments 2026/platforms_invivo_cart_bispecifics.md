## Q1. In-vivo CAR-T clinical data 2025-2026, and whether any platform is species-agnostic / has dog data

### Takeaway
In-vivo CAR generation is now clinical in at least 7 sponsor programs; the only peer-reviewed efficacy is myeloma (ESO-T01, 4/5 OR, 3 sCR, n=5) and the only lymphoma efficacy is a Chinese targeted-lentivirus series (dose level 2: 6/6 ORR, 5/6 CR, median follow-up 2.3 mo) plus a 4-7 patient first-in-human CD8-targeted **LNP** series in B-cell lymphoma that the project's record does not contain (record had LNP CAR in humans as "SLE only"). No in-vivo CAR program has published dog data with a CAR payload; the only canine datum remains Umoja's GFP VivoVec beagle study already in the record, and the Oct-2026 reviews contain **no claim that any vector is species-agnostic**.

- **Veterinary in-vivo CAR program:** none found again (searches: "canine in vivo CAR-T", "veterinary in vivo CAR-T", Oct 2026 news). Consistent with the record's NOT FOUND (docs/universe/SWEEP_dog_programs.md:35).

### Inferences
- The modality has crossed from "first-in-human" to "multiple sponsors with interim cohorts" inside the window, but **durability is still unmeasured**: the longest follow-up datum found anywhere is a single LB2501/LIVTO relapse at 9 months and "all DL2 responses ongoing at 2.3 months". For a 10-year-durability model, in-vivo CAR-T remains readiness C (human clinical-stage) with **no durability input**, exactly as the record has it.
- The new CD22-CAR+RACR vector (UB-VV400) and the CD5-targeted LNP are the two most dog-relevant additions, because they attack the record's identified dog barrier (no expansion/persistence) with a drug-controlled expansion switch and with a binder class (CD5) for which validated anti-canine clones already exist. Both are preclinical, so under the project's admissibility rule they are **modality-supporting, not admissible agents in themselves**.
- The record's "mRNA-LNP in-vivo CAR = SLE only, no cancer data" line is now out of date: there is a first-in-human B-cell lymphoma LNP series (ASCO 2026 7014). Strength is CONF with unretrievable efficacy numbers, so the row should change from "no cancer data found" to "first-in-human lymphoma series exists; numbers not retrievable".

### Gaps
- Efficacy/durability/safety numbers for ASCO 2026 abstract 7014 (CD8-tLNP in lymphoma): publisher 403; patient count conflicts (4 vs 7).
- No clinical readout exists for CPTX2309, INT2104 or UB-VV111 as of 2026-10-31 despite repeated searching; absence of a readout, not a negative result.
- Whether any in-vivo targeting binder (anti-CD3, CD5, CD7, CD8) cross-reacts with the canine orthologue: still unanswered by any published source.
- The BMC Cancer systematic review of in-vivo CAR-T clinical trials (published 2026-10-16, doi 10.1186/s12885-026-17165-x) could not be retrieved (Springer redirect to an auth endpoint); its pooled numbers are unread. POST-SWEEP item left unverified.

## Q2. Off-the-shelf allogeneic CAR-T / CAR-NK and armed T cells in B-cell lymphoma; any canine allogeneic product

### Takeaway
The single most important new allogeneic datum in the window is Allogene's **ALPHA3 interim futility analysis (13 Apr 2026)**: allogeneic CD19 CAR-T (cema-cel) as **first-line consolidation in MRD-positive LBCL** cleared MRD in 7/12 (58.3%) vs 2/12 (16.7%) with observation, with **no CRS, no ICANS and no GVHD at any grade** — the first randomised human evidence that an off-the-shelf product can clear minimal residual disease, which is the exact function the canine model needs after induction. No canine allogeneic CAR product has published data; the two dog xeno-CAR sites remain on hold.

### Cited Findings
- **cema-cel, ALPHA3 (phase 2, pivotal, randomised, 1L consolidation of MRD+ LBCL)**: interim futility analysis reported 2026-04-13; **MRD clearance at day 45 in 7/12 (58.3%) cema-cel vs 2/12 (16.7%) observation**; **97.7% reduction in plasma ctDNA** vs +26.6% in the observation arm; **no CRS, no ICANS, no GVHD of any grade; no treatment-related serious adverse events; 10/12 managed outpatient**; trial continues to ~220 randomised MRD+ patients with EFS as primary endpoint, primary results expected 2027 — [OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/cema-cel-improves-mrd-clearance-as-first-line-consolidation-in-large-b-cell-lymphoma); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/allogenes-shelf-car-t-erases-lingering-lymphoma-cells-key-early-win); [AJMC](https://www.ajmc.com/view/early-alpha3-data-could-signal-shift-to-frontline-use-of-car-t-in-lbcl); company 8-K/Q1 2026 update — [Allogene IR](https://ir.allogene.com/news-releases/news-release-details/allogene-therapeutics-reports-first-quarter-2026-financial). Stage: **phase 2 interim (n=24 analysed)**. Grade WEB/CONF (company + trade press; no peer-reviewed paper found). **NOT IN RECORD** (grep "cema", "ALPHA3": no hit; "Allogene" appears across the record only as the company in other contexts).
- A market report states the FDA granted cema-cel **RMAT and Fast Track** after the interim data — [PRISM MarketView](https://prismmarketview.com/allogenes-alpha3-data-and-fda-designations-sharpen-the-case-for-cema-cel-in-lymphoma/) (WEB, not confirmed against an FDA source; treat as unverified).
- **Universal/off-the-shelf CAR-T state of the art (POST-SWEEP review)**: *Med Rev* 2026-10, PMID 42924291, [doi 10.1515/mr-2026-0017](https://doi.org/10.1515/mr-2026-0017) — the field has moved from proof-of-concept to early-phase human trials; the two named unresolved hurdles are **durable CAR-T persistence** and **innate + adaptive immune rejection (HvGD)** alongside GVHD mitigation. Review (no new numbers). NOT IN RECORD.
- **In-vivo CAR-NK as an off-the-shelf-equivalent route** (no donor cells needed at all): Nat Biotechnol 2026-10-13, PMID 42870042 (numbers in Q1). POST-SWEEP, preclinical. NOT IN RECORD.
- **Dogs**: no canine allogeneic or xenogeneic CAR product data published in the window. University of Missouri's "LEAH xCAR-T" and University of Minnesota's xenogeneic CD20 CAR-T remain listed **on hold** — [Missouri current clinical trials](https://vhc.missouri.edu/small-animal-hospital/oncology/clinical-trials/current-clinical-trials/); [Minnesota CAR-T page](https://vetmed.umn.edu/departments/centers-and-programs/clinical-investigation-center/current-clinical-trials/car-t-cell) (REG-like institutional pages). ALREADY IN RECORD (docs/universe/SWEEP_dog_programs.md, "Missouri trial page 2026-10-04: LEAH xCAR-T ON HOLD; Minnesota ... ON HOLD").
- **Dog CAR-T outcome datum found only in lay press (NEW, weak)**: a Newsweek feature on the LEAH Labs / University of Missouri NCI-funded PET-imageable CAR-T trial states **two dogs** with advanced lymphoma were treated with CAR-T **injected directly into the lymph nodes**, disease went "from measurable to microscopic in about a week and a half", **both reached complete remission, and both recurred months later**; a third dog was hoped for in February; no adverse events described — [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/dog-cancer-almost-back-normal-after-pioneering-new-treatment-2023820) (WEB; **the page carries no date in the fetched text**, so even the year is unconfirmed). **NOT IN RECORD** as an efficacy datum (the record has the LEAH/Missouri program and NCI DOGS supplement but states results unpublished: docs/universe/SWEEP_dog_programs.md). If true it is the **first reported complete remissions after CAR-T in dogs**, with relapse within months — directionally consistent with the record's persistence problem. Must not be used as a model input at this grade.
- **Armed T cells (BATs) in lymphoma**: no new trial in any species found in the window; the record's conclusion stands (docs/universe/SWEEP_bispecific.md section 7; docs/universe/SWEEP_near_future_cell.md:124-129).

### Inferences
- ALPHA3 reframes the allogeneic product's role from "salvage substitute for autologous CAR-T" to **MRD consolidation after chemotherapy-induced remission**, which maps directly onto the project's canine architecture (CHOP-like induction, then a consolidation agent against residual/dormant disease). The absence of CRS/ICANS/GVHD at the consolidation dose is the mechanistically relevant part for a dog where Flu/Cy support is impractical (record: PMID 38573683).
- The dog path for allogeneic remains the weakest link: two sites on hold, one company (LEAH), no published data. The modality is clinical-stage in humans (admissible as class C) but has **no stated, dated dog path beyond the paused trials**.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed publication of the ALPHA3 interim; all numbers are company/trade-press.
- cema-cel persistence/durability (how long the allogeneic cells last, whether MRD clearance converts to EFS): not available until 2027 primary analysis.
- Whether the Newsweek dog CAR-T remissions correspond to the LEAH xeno product or an autologous construct, their dates, doses, and relapse intervals: unknown; institutional pages give no results.

## Q3. B-cell bispecifics: new approvals/label expansions 2025-2026, durable-remission data, and any canine engager

### Takeaway
Two regulatory moves in the window are new to the record — **epcoritamab + R2 for R/R FL (traditional approval, November 2025; PFS HR 0.21)** and a **subcutaneous mosunetuzumab label (reported December 2025)** — and three POST-SWEEP peer-reviewed papers add first-line and CNS numbers (glofit-pola-R-CHP 24-month PFS 95%; glofitamab + polatuzumab in R/R CNS lymphoma ORR 100%/CR 89%, n=9). Odronextamab is still not FDA-approved (second CRL) but is approved in the EU. There is still **no canine-reactive or caninised CD3 engager anywhere**; the only movement on the dog side is reagent/platform work.

### Cited Findings
- **Epcoritamab + lenalidomide + rituximab (R2), R/R FL — traditional FDA approval November 2025.** FDA Approval Summary (POST-SWEEP publication, *Clin Cancer Res* 2026-10-01, PMID 42820870, [doi 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1867](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1867)): based on EPCORE FL-1 (N=488, randomised, open label, **fixed duration**); **PFS HR 0.21 (95% CI 0.13-0.33), p<0.0001**; median PFS **not reached** (95% CI 22-NR) vs 11.2 months for R2 alone; **ORR 96% vs 81%** (first 232 patients, IRC-assessed). NOT IN RECORD (grep "EPCORE FL-1", "tafasitamab", "approv" in SWEEP_bispecific.md: the record's bispecific sweep contains efficacy tables but **no approval/label status section at all**).
- **Subcutaneous mosunetuzumab**: a regulatory comparison table lists an IV Lunsumio label (December 2022, accelerated, R/R FL >=2 prior lines) **and a subcutaneous option dated December 2025** — [Medscape, CD20xCD3 bispecifics: converging efficacy, diverging regulatory paths](https://www.medscape.com/c99/p10/cd20xcd3-bispecific-antibodies-b-cell-lymphoma-converging-2026a10013zt) (article dated ~late Oct 2026; **page returned HTTP 402 on direct fetch, so this is a search-snippet-level claim** — treat the December 2025 SC date as unverified). NOT IN RECORD. Corroborating peer-reviewed use of **subcutaneous mosunetuzumab** in a phase 1/2 trial: PMID 42832423 (below).
- **Odronextamab**: **not FDA-approved**; FDA issued a complete response letter for R/R FL (a second CRL, reportedly on a site inspection), while the agent **is approved in the EU as Orspono** — [Medscape table](https://www.medscape.com/c99/p10/cd20xcd3-bispecific-antibodies-b-cell-lymphoma-converging-2026a10013zt) (snippet-level); [ASH Clinical News](https://ashpublications.org/ashclinicalnews/news/8597/Odronextamab-Back-In-Front-of-FDA-for-Approval); [Targeted Oncology, second FDA denial](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/for-a-second-time-fda-denies-approval-of-odronextamab-in-lymphoma). Record has odronextamab efficacy/PK (docs/universe/SWEEP_bispecific.md:38,42,106,178,214) but **no regulatory status**; EU approval NOT IN RECORD.
- **POST-SWEEP, peer-reviewed, first-line and CNS numbers (all NOT IN RECORD):**
  - **Glofitamab + pola-R-CHP, newly diagnosed DLBCL IPI>=2, phase 2, n=41** — *Blood* 2026-10-06, PMID 42837325, [doi 10.1182/blood.2026034588](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2026034588): **best CR 95% (39/41, 95% CI 83-99)**; both end-of-therapy PRs converted to CR without intervention; median follow-up 23.9 months, median PFS not reached, **24-month PFS 95% (95% CI 89-100)**; **CRS 4/41 (10%), all grade 1; no ICANS** (glofitamab deliberately delayed to cycle 3).
  - **Subcutaneous mosunetuzumab + polatuzumab, 1L LBCL in unfit/frail older patients, phase 1/2, n=101 at full dose** — *Blood* 2026-10-05, PMID 42832423, [doi 10.1182/blood.2026035116](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2026035116): median age **81**; ORR at end of treatment **61.4%** (CR 56.4%); best ORR 80.2%, best CR 66.3%; median follow-up **36.5 months**, median DOR and OS not reached; **2-year PFS 52.2%, OS 62.4%**; **CRS 30.6%, predominantly grade 1**; infection-related mortality observed. ## Q4. Approaches to quiescent/dormant lymphoma cells and MRD clearance with clinical evidence

### Takeaway
The only genuinely new clinical evidence in the window that bears on residual/dormant disease is **MRD-directed consolidation**: ALPHA3's randomised MRD-clearance signal (Q2) and the epcoritamab MRD/ctDNA analyses. Nothing new was found that *directly* targets quiescence (no wake-then-kill, niche-release or pump-independent agent with clinical data beyond what the record already holds), and the drug-pump-high quiescent progenitor escape remains unaddressed by any agent in this window.

### Cited Findings
- **Allogeneic CAR-T clears MRD in first remission (randomised, interim)**: cema-cel ALPHA3 — MRD clearance 58.3% vs 16.7%, ctDNA -97.7% vs +26.6%, no CRS/ICANS/GVHD (sources in Q2). Phase 2 interim. NOT IN RECORD. This is the strongest *clinical* MRD-clearance datum found in the window and is mechanistically MHC-independent and not division-gated (T-cell perforin/granzyme kill), i.e. it does not require the residual cell to cycle.
- **Engager MRD depth and durability (ALREADY IN RECORD)**: epcoritamab monotherapy R/R FL, MRD negativity by PBMC and ctDNA confirming deep, durable responses — *Blood Adv*, PMID 42431612 (record: docs/universe/SWEEP_bispecific.md line 28ff, "most MRD-evaluable patients reaching MRD negativity by cycle 3 day 1"). Odronextamab ctDNA at C4D15 predicting PFS — PMID 40902079 (record).
- **ctDNA/MRD methodology for lymphoma** (POST-SWEEP-adjacent, 2026-09-22): *Blood Adv* PMID 42284698, analysis of cell-free DNA in lymphomas from collection to MRD monitoring — review of the assay layer that any canine MRD-consolidation design would need. NOT IN RECORD.
- **Genomic drivers of relapse/refractoriness** (POST-SWEEP): whole-genome + transcriptome of R/R NHL in a multicentre precision-oncology cohort — *Br J Haematol* 2026-10-23, PMID 42925120 (numbers not extracted). NOT IN RECORD.
- **No new wake-the-dormant clinical data**: searches for quiescent/dormant/MRD x lymphoma 2026-09-15 to 2026-10-31 returned only the items above plus CLL/MCL management reviews (PMID 42566728, 42555305). The record's dormancy sweep (docs/universe/SWEEP_wake_dormant.md) remains the current state: CXCR4 antagonists mobilise blasts 3.3-58x but **no survival benefit** (plerixafor+MEC terminated for futility, 28282031; randomised motixafortide + HiDAC in AML first remission **null**, median RFS 10.3 vs 11.5 months, P=.98, PMID 41980027); no lymphoma-specific priming study exists.
- **In-vivo CAR-T does not solve quiescence either, and the new reviews say why**: the production-rate framework names **antigen-encounter timing mismatch** as a constraint — if CAR-T are generated and then decay (mRNA-LNP: back to baseline within 2-3 days per PMC13587011; 7-10 days per the record's press source) the probability of meeting a rarely-cycling, niche-sequestered cell in the active window is low — *Front Immunol* PMID 42827775, [doi 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1939127](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1939127); *J Control Release* PMID 42962824, [doi 10.1016/j.jconrel.2026.115511](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jconrel.2026.115511). Reviews (mechanistic framing, no new numbers).

### Inferences
- The window's net effect on the dormant-cell escape is a **shift in strategy rather than a new agent**: instead of waking dormant cells into cycle (repeatedly null in AML trials), the clinical field is now testing *persistent or repeatable antigen-directed pressure applied while the burden is at MRD level* (ALPHA3 consolidation; fixed-duration engager with MRD endpoints). For the canine model this favours an agent with a long functional window (integrating-vector in-vivo CAR, allogeneic consolidation, or repeated engager dosing) over a chemosensitiser.
- A transient mRNA-LNP CAR is the **worst fit** for a quiescent reservoir on these grounds, unless redosed on a schedule matched to the dormant-to-cycling switching rate (record's anchors: 0.0036-0.0069/day).

### Gaps
- No clinical agent with evidence of killing non-cycling, efflux-pump-high lymphoid progenitors specifically; nothing new found in the window.
- ALPHA3's MRD clearance is measured by ctDNA/clonoSEQ-type assays in blood; whether it reflects clearance of a tissue-resident quiescent reservoir is unknown.
