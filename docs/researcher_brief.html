Research brief · comparative oncology

The durability gap in canine histiocytic sarcoma

The field has the targets and, increasingly, the tools. What is missing is a durability-specific program: a prevention framing, one under-explored genotype-anchored target, and an integrated map that says which experiment to run first. This brief is a synthesis on top of an active field — not a discovery.

Scope predisposed-breed HS, all sitesStatus hypothesis-generatingNot veterinary advice

Genotype-matched targeting of canine HS is already in dogs (trametinib Phase I). The additive contribution here is three things the treatment-focused literature leaves implicit: (1) reframe from treatment to maintenance-at-emergence / prevention; (2) test the most genotype-anchored target nobody has connected to this disease — MTAP → PRMT5 (and the parallel MAT2A route); (3) an integrated escape × site × genotype durability ledger with a value-of-information ranking of experiments.

01 · orientationWhat the field already has, and what is additive

Stated plainly so the brief earns its place: nearly every component below is already owned by an active group. The contribution is integration plus two under-explored specifics.

Already pursued

  • MAPK/MEK targeting + PTPN11 driver — trametinib Phase I in dogs [1]; PTPN11/SHP2 gain-of-function in Bernese HS [2]; ERK/AKT inhibitor combinations in HS lines [3].
  • Molecular subtyping & combinations — HS transcriptome, multi-target + checkpoint proposals, Aurora+MAPK synergy [4]; subtype-directed precision-medicine call [5].
  • ctDNA monitoring — plasma PTPN11 assay validated in canine HS [6]; commercial canine platform.
  • Genotype-matched switch-on-reroute — demonstrated in human histiocytoses, incl. resistance/transformation [7].

Additive here

  • Prevention framing — continuous, genotype-matched maintenance to suppress a second primary in the predisposed host; an adjuvant/RFS trial design, not treatment of bulk disease.
  • MTAP → PRMT5 (and MAT2A) for HS — the most genotype-anchored maintenance target, unconnected to canine HS in the literature (zero hits). Cheap to test. Caveat: acquired PRMT5i resistance via MAPK reprogramming is documented, and confers collateral MEK sensitivity — a defined second line, not an open exit.
  • Durability ledger + VoI — sites × mechanisms × escapes × genotypes in one accounting, with a ranked list of which measurement most reduces uncertainty.
  • Quantified confidence — P(10-yr) per tier with intervals, calibrated to the adjuvant CCNU baseline [8].

02 · do these firstExperiments, ranked by value of information

Ordered so the earliest entries are the cheapest and most decisive. The ranking is not editorial: in the durability model the dominant uncertainty is the second-primary emergence rate, and the largest single unknown-vs-cost gap is MTAP status — so those lead.

1

MTAP status in canine HS → PRMT5i sensitivity in MTAP-null lines

IHC / copy-number for MTAP (and CDKN2A co-deletion) on archived HS tissue — no live animal needed. If an MTAP-null subset exists, test an MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibitor (TNG462 / TNG456-class [9,10]; BMS-986504 drove regressions in MTAP-null sarcoma incl. MPNST [11]) on MTAP-null canine HS lines. This target is genotype-anchored — the deletion cannot be undone, making it the most durable tier, though not absolute (acquired resistance is documented, with collateral MEK sensitivity as a defined second line). The parallel MAT2A route (AG-270 class, Phase I complete) exploits the same deletion. Weeks, not years, and it either opens the arm or rules it out.

2

Second-primary emergence-rate cohort

Retrospective/prospective recurrence-free interval in cured localized HS, stratified by genotype. This is the dominant term in the durability model's uncertainty (variance share ~0.5–0.9), so it tightens every downstream estimate more than any drug screen. Anchors the whole prevention argument to a measured rate rather than an extrapolation from the CCNU baseline [8].

3

Adjuvant maintenance trial (the prevention reframe)

Post-local-control, genotype-matched continuous maintenance (MEK for the ~59% MAPK tier), primary endpoint recurrence-free interval vs the historical CCNU control (~243 d DFI [8]), with plasma-PTPN11 ctDNA-guided switch [6]. Confirms the mechanism the whole plan rests on — that maintenance-at-emergence prevents a second primary — which no current trial tests.

trial designRFS endpoint
4

CNS: local-delivery penetration + on-biopsy target engagement

Measure drug exposure delivered into the resection cavity (CED / implant) for the brain-local tier — at that site the model's dominant uncertainty flips from emergence to reach. In parallel, confirm MEK target engagement on tumour biopsy, the open item the trametinib trial itself flagged [1]. Radiation already achieves parenchymal CR in a predisposed breed [12]; the documented failure mode is CSF seeding [12].

5

Tumour-informed ctDNA MRD cadence for canine HS

Extend the validated PTPN11 plasma assay [6] to a tumour-informed MRD readout and establish the sampling cadence that catches a reroute inside the switch window. Single-test sensitivity (~43–77%) is the parameter to beat; serial cadence and personalized panels are the levers. This is what converts "surveillance-dependent" from a caveat into a protocol.

Escape coverage

Across the 12 enumerated escape routes (pathway reactivation, target mutation, parallel-pathway bypass, lineage independence, immune evasion, death-program resistance, dormancy, DNA repair, second-primary seeding, and the metabolic/ferroptosis axes): 7 rest on drugs measured to kill canine HS cells, 2 on close-cousin transfers, 1 model-derived, 2 structural — and 0 on a bare assumption. Coverage is model-based; durability robustness is what the ledger above grades.

04 · trial sketchThe prevention study, concretely

  • Population. Resected and/or irradiated localized HS in predisposed breeds, in complete response after local control, genotyped once from tumour tissue (NGS panel + MTAP IHC).
  • Stratify. MTAP-null → PRMT5i arm (the locked substratum); MAPK → MEK; PTEN → PI3K; CDKN2A/RB1-intact → CDK4/6; none → monitored floor.
  • Intervention. Continuous, tolerable maintenance from CR, dosed to the maintenance bar (≈25× below the tumour-shrinking exposure, so the trametinib underdosing concern does not bind [1]); per-dog TDM; synergistic pairing where the ceiling is tight [3].
  • Surveillance. Serial plasma-PTPN11 ctDNA [6]; on a confirmed rise, switch to the next matched anchor per the genotype tree.
  • Endpoint. Recurrence-free interval vs the CCNU historical control (~243 d DFI; ~568 d OS [8]). Powered to detect the shift a genotype-matched, continuously-dosable, non-cumulative maintenance predicts over blunt cytotoxic adjuvant.

05 · limitsWhat this brief is not

Honest boundary

This is synthesis and prioritization, not new bench data. The probabilities rest on documented but assumed priors and a single-institution cell-line basis; no maintenance-at-emergence claim is proven in a dog; several inputs (CNS penetration, emergence rate, chronic tolerability, canine PRMT5i sensitivity) are unmeasured. A group would recompute with its own data. The value is a map plus one cheap decisive experiment — the MTAP stain — that can save a program from pursuing the wrong slice.
